E0 fixed `_isolated_index_copy` (used by `preserve_candidate`) with `shutil.copy2`, and added a deterministic regression test (tests/unit/test_candidate_preservation.py::test_isolated_index_copy_preserves_live_index_mtime_not_fresh_copy_time).
