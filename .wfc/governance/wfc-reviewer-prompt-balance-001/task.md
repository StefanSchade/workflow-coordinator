The instruction to integrate:

Evaluate simplicity and maintainability alongside correctness and compliance.

Actively consider whether the same authorized objectives and guarantees can be achieved with a materially simpler solution. Look for unnecessary abstractions, redundant safeguards, duplicated responsibilities, avoidable state, and recurring operational or maintenance burdens.

Distinguish one-time implementation and verification effort from ongoing complexity costs. Extensive implementation and review effort is acceptable when it produces a better long-term result.

A blocking maintainability finding must identify a concrete cost or defect and a feasible simpler alternative. Do not block on stylistic preferences.

Do not silently introduce new requirements. If an authorized acceptance criterion itself appears disproportionate or unnecessary, flag it for maintainer consideration rather than waiving it or adding further mechanisms to satisfy it.
