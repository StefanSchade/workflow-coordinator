EXACT DESIGN QUESTION:

    What trusted mechanical qualification information must enter
    replay-deterministic convergence-fact derivation, and at which authority
    boundary, so that failed required evidence cannot qualify a convergence
    cycle without creating a mutable shadow ledger or collapsing semantic and
    mechanical authority?

The currently falsified simplified contract was effectively:

    ConvergenceFact = f(Run/Hop/Finding semantic history, qualified_cycle_limit)

The empirical evidence shows that this input set is insufficient for at least
the `required_evidence_failed` case. The revision must determine the
smallest coherent replacement.

CANDIDATE SOLUTION FAMILIES TO FALSIFY -- the design must explicitly consider
and compare at least these families, and must not assume any is already
chosen:

A. Durable qualification inside Run/Hop semantics. Persist a trusted
   qualification fact associated with the Hop so Run history alone remains
   sufficient for replay. Challenge: authority ownership; wire/schema impact;
   legacy compatibility; whether trusted execution evidence is being
   improperly collapsed into semantic agent history; exact moment at which
   the fact becomes durable; crash/recovery behavior; whether this creates
   duplicated truth.

B. Derivation over semantic Run history PLUS trusted execution evidence. Keep
   semantic Run/Hop/Finding history and trusted mechanical execution evidence
   separate. Conceptually:
   ConvergenceFact = f(Run history, trusted execution evidence, qualified_cycle_limit).
   Challenge: deterministic Hop/invocation correlation; missing, duplicate,
   inconsistent, stale, or partial evidence; crash/recovery; replay
   requirements; checkpoint construction; whether execution-evidence becomes
   an authority source or only a trusted predicate input; how old/legacy Runs
   behave; whether this preserves the intended semantic/mechanical boundary.

C. Evidence failure prevents semantic convergence records from becoming
   authoritative. Change reduction/publication semantics so a failed
   required-evidence evaluator Hop does not leave convergence records
   capable of later qualification. Challenge: whether this loses truthful
   historical information; transactional ordering; Finding delta semantics;
   current failed-evidence routing behavior; compatibility with existing
   Slice-1 authority and replay; whether it incorrectly rewrites the meaning
   of accepted agent output.

Other solution: a different solution may be selected only if it is materially
smaller or more coherent and is explicitly compared against A/B/C. No
solution may be chosen merely because it is easiest to code.

KEY CONCEPTUAL DISTINCTION -- the revision must reason explicitly about two
existing evidence surfaces, and must decide (not assume) their relationship:

Semantic history: Hop identity, role, coverage claims, convergence
assessments, Finding lifecycle, applied route, semantic outcome.

Trusted mechanical evidence: Coordinator-executed required evidence,
pass/fail/error/not_run, command identity, trusted execution Observation,
invocation/Hop correlation.

Do not assume these surfaces must merge. Do not assume they must remain
completely independent. Decide the smallest justified relationship necessary
for `ConvergenceFact`. A qualified convergence cycle is potentially a
composite derived fact: semantic predicate AND trusted mechanical
qualification predicate. The design must decide whether that is the correct
model.

REQUIRED DESIGN INVARIANTS -- whichever solution is selected, preserve or
explicitly revise with justification:

1. No shadow ledger: no second mutable convergence counter; no separate
   mutable goalpost truth.
2. Replay determinism: same authoritative input set => same `ConvergenceFact`.
3. Fail closed: absent, ambiguous, inconsistent, untrusted, or uncorrelated
   required qualification must never cause a cycle to qualify.
4. No semantic authority from evidence machinery: a test pass must not
   resolve a Finding or constitute semantic acceptance by itself.
5. No agent authority over trusted qualification: an agent's own evidence
   reference or prose cannot self-certify the mechanical predicate.
6. Stable correlation: qualification must correlate to the exact Hop /
   invocation whose semantic assessment is being considered.
7. No historical rewrite: old Runs are not silently migrated/backfilled.
8. Slice-1 historical acceptance remains intact unless the revision proves an
   actual Slice-1 contract contradiction. Do not reopen Slice 1 merely
   because Slice 2 needs a richer read model.
9. Checkpoint remains derived: checkpoint projection must not become
   independent authority.
10. No premature Slice-3/4 behavior: this revision may clarify future
    integration but must not smuggle in escape routing, diagnosis/reframe, or
    generation advancement.
11. Truthful failed-evidence history: distinguish "the evaluator semantically
    claimed a counterexample" from "the Coordinator's required evidence
    qualified that Hop" if the selected design requires both facts.
12. Minimal scope: prefer a narrow revised contract over generalized event
    sourcing, provenance frameworks, or a new persistence substrate.

IMPORTANT EXISTING ARCHITECTURE FACTS -- inspect and account for current
behavior rather than reasoning abstractly:

* trusted evidence observations are already durably represented outside
  `Run`: `operator.py`'s `run_serial_hops` already writes per-attempt,
  per-Hop/invocation `trusted_observations` (pass/fail/error/not_run status,
  command identity, argv digest, timeouts) to `execution-evidence.json`
  (`{"schema_version": 3, "attempts": [...]}`), correlated by `hop_id` and
  `invocation_id`.
* `MechanicalFacts.required_evidence_failed` is available during reduction
  (`vertical.py` computes it per Hop from `observations` and passes it into
  `reduce_result`), but it is transient: it is never written onto the
  persisted `Hop`/`HopSummary` record.
* required evidence failure can select a configured `failed_evidence_route`
  (`reducer.py`, gated on `phase.review.authority is ReviewAuthority.GATE`
  and a `goto` route) -- but only as a routing effect at reduction time, with
  no durable qualification fact left behind for later derivation.
* semantic Finding deltas/assessment history and trusted evidence
  qualification currently have distinct ownership and distinct persistence
  homes (`Run`/`Hop` vs. `execution-evidence.json`).
* checkpoint is derived and not independent authority.
* Run/Hop `evidence_refs` are agent-attributed references and are not
  equivalent to trusted observations.
* the Coordinator already persists execution-evidence records containing
  per-Hop/invocation trusted observations, as above.

Do not invent a new evidence system if an existing durable surface is
sufficient.
