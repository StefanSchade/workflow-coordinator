H001-created current-Run OPEN Finding and, if its own independent
verification confirms the corresponding blocker is in fact already
satisfied, resolve it; if `review` instead finds a genuine defect, it opens
or leaves open a Finding and routes `correct`, and `rereview` may later
resolve it once a subsequent `correct` Hop has verifiably fixed it. Both
`review` and `rereview` are therefore granted the same minimal
`finding_authority: [resolve]` -- not `residual`, not `supersede`, not
`reopen`, not broader. This mirrors the fix already applied by the S3-003
successor Commission after
docs/backlog/BUG-WFC-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-GATE-FINDING-AUTHORITY.adoc was
discovered (there, the profile began directly at `rereview` with no
`implement`/`review` phase at all, so only `rereview` needed the grant; this
Commission's topology differs by having both an `implement` Hop that can
precede `review`'s first encounter with a Finding and a `correct` Hop that
can precede `rereview`'s, so both gates need the identical grant). Stable
`S4-IRxxx` identifiers are durable review-artifact prose, dispositioned by
writing directly into the durable review artifact -- never by (and never
requiring) a WFC Finding-ledger `resolve` operation. Do not create a
synthetic S4-001 Run-native Finding merely to mirror an S4-IR identifier or
to have something to `resolve`.

TOPOLOGY: `implement -> review -> {accept: complete, correct: correct}`,
`correct -> rereview -> {accept: complete, correct: correct}`. Initial phase:
`implement`. `max_autonomous_hops: 10` is a CEILING, not a target; stop
immediately on valid independent acceptance or stronger preemption. Do not
add a fixed review-iteration cap and do not manufacture corrections merely to
consume budget.
