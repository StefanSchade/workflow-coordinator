======================================================================
CORE P2 PROPERTY
======================================================================

Make worker-vs-review category mistakes structurally unrepresentable, not
merely rejected after the fact by the reducer.

Worker/corrector semantic family -- `WorkResult`:

  status: completed | blocked

plus the genuinely applicable existing semantic/evidence/effect fields
required to preserve current behavior (see SCOPE OF WHAT MUST BE
PRESERVED below). A WorkResult MUST NOT be able to express
`changes_required` or any other review-only verdict -- absent from the
type and the provider schema, not merely rejected later by the reducer.

Review/rereview semantic family -- `ReviewVerdict`:

  verdict: accept | changes_required

plus Finding proposals/updates, evidence, summary, and only those
currently required fields that genuinely apply to review semantics. A
ReviewVerdict MUST NOT expose worker-only status vocabulary merely
because the old generic RoutingResult did.

Every other still-supported invocation family remains on the existing
generic `RoutingResult` contract unless this task explicitly says
otherwise. Concretely, confirmed by source inspection at commissioning
time: every phase with `phase.convergence_role is not
ConvergenceRole.NONE` (Slice 4: CORRECTOR, DIAGNOSIS, REFRAME,
STRATEGY_GATE, EVALUATOR) is OUT OF SCOPE for P2's new kinds and MUST
continue selecting today's generic contract, unaffected in dispatched
schema, parser, or reducer input. P2's `WorkResult`/`ReviewVerdict`
mapping applies only where `phase.convergence_role is
ConvergenceRole.NONE`: `WorkResult` for `phase.role is WorkflowRole.WORKER`
with no review descriptor; `ReviewVerdict` for `phase.role is
WorkflowRole.INDEPENDENT_REVIEWER` with a review descriptor present.

`NewFindingAuthoring`/`ExistingFindingUpdate` generalization beyond this

======================================================================

outside of consuming the new types correctly in place of the generic one.
Reducer processing remains the stateful authority; semantic authorization
must not move into the adapter or parser.
