IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY
=======================

Implement exactly the accepted P3 semantics:

* Existing-Finding citation and state-changing transition are separate,
  structural result variants. A citation is evidence-only, carries only the
  existing-Finding identity and evidence references, needs no
  `finding_authority`, and cannot express a disposition.
* Citation must not carry `proposed_state`, `successor_ref`, or
  `new_material_evidence`. A state-changing transition retains the existing
  disposition-authority checks. `successor_ref` is structurally available
  only on the supersede transition form.
* Leave new-Finding authorship unchanged. Preserve the existing reducer's
  authority, atomicity, cycle, and obligation checks. Leave the
  CORRECTOR-only tracked-obligation check unchanged unless preserving it is
  structurally impossible; if that is genuinely unclear, escalate scope
  rather than deciding a wider rule.
* Add P3 `result_contract_version: 3` only for `WORK_RESULT` and
  `REVIEW_VERDICT`. Versions 1/2 retain their supported semantics. Unknown
  or unadmitted `(kind, version)` pairs fail closed, including
  `(GENERIC_ROUTING_RESULT, 3)`.
* In `adapter.py` and `prompt.py`, make only the smallest correct kind-aware
  admission change for those two v3 pairs. Do not globally turn `{1, 2}`
  into `{1, 2, 3}` if that admits generic routing v3. Do not change prompt
  content or composition beyond what correct kind/version admission requires.
