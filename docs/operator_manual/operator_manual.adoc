This manual brings the existing operator guidance together in ten
chapters. Follow the reading path below for the current workflow,
troubleshooting and examples. Preparation, self-development and recovery
retain their explicit normative section owners; this entry is navigation.
The five legacy root paths retain compatibility pointers; current reading
uses the section owners below. The transitional Operator Guide paths have
been retired.

[[reading-path]]
== Reading path

. New to WFC and applying it to your own, unrelated repository rather than
  developing WFC itself? Start with the
  link:ch09_consumer_quickstart/01_quickstart.adoc#consumer-quickstart[consumer quickstart]
  in the link:ch09_consumer_quickstart/_chapter.adoc#consumer-quickstart-chapter[consumer quickstart chapter]
  below instead of the rest of this reading path.
. Read link:ch01_orientation/01_model.adoc[the mental model] and
  link:ch01_orientation/02_boundaries.adoc[execution boundaries] below.
. For Commission inputs, authority, authoring and review, read the
  link:ch02_commissioning/_chapter.adoc#commissioning[commissioning chapter]
  below. For trusted publication, evidence and acceptance, read
  link:ch04_evidence/01_acceptance.adoc#trusted-acceptance[trusted acceptance and correction routing].
. For the self-development specialization, read the normative
  link:ch05_self_development/01_topology_budget.adoc[self-development topology, preparation, budget and enforcement layers]
  and link:ch05_self_development/02_promotion.adoc[Development salvage and Runtime promotion]
  in the link:ch05_self_development/_chapter.adoc#self-development[self-development chapter] below.
. For preparation through authorization, read the normative
  link:ch03_running/01_preparation.adoc[Run-preparation section], including
  its informative product-Front-Door command and self-development substrate
  distinction.
. For launch, monitoring and stop interpretation, read
  link:ch03_running/02_launch_status.adoc[launch, status and terminal states]
  in the link:ch03_running/_chapter.adoc#running[running chapter] below.
. For evidence and archiving, use
  link:ch04_evidence/02_archive.adoc#archive[ARCHIVE],
  link:ch04_evidence/02_archive.adoc#semantic-ledger[semantic ledger curation], and
  link:ch04_evidence/02_archive.adoc#evidence-architecture[evidence architecture]
  in the link:ch04_evidence/_chapter.adoc#evidence[evidence chapter] below.
. For a stopped Run, use the normative
  link:ch06_recovery/01_outcomes.adoc#recovery-outcomes[recovery outcomes and exact salvage]
  in the link:ch06_recovery/_chapter.adoc#recovery[recovery chapter] below;
  it leads to the informative
  link:ch06_recovery/02_successors.adoc[current successor procedure]
  and optional link:ch06_recovery/03_reference_case.adoc[historical cases].
. For existing operating diagnoses, consult
  link:ch07_diagnostics/01_troubleshooting.adoc#troubleshooting[troubleshooting]
  in the link:ch07_diagnostics/_chapter.adoc#diagnostics[diagnostics chapter].
. Follow the complete
  link:ch08_examples/03_commission_to_archive.adoc#commission-to-archive[Commission-to-archive walkthrough]
  in the link:ch08_examples/_chapter.adoc#examples[examples chapter]. Its
  historical facts and illustrative operations are labelled separately;
  the link:ch08_examples/01_worked_reference.adoc#worked-reference[older stopped reference]
  and link:ch08_examples/02_command_flow.adoc#command-flow[command index]
  remain available.
. For an external agent orchestrating WFC above a single Run -- decomposing
  a larger goal into bounded Commissions, launching, monitoring, verifying
  and integrating each one -- read the informative
  link:ch10_level1_orchestration/01_operating_pattern.adoc[Level-1 operating pattern]
  and its
  link:ch10_level1_orchestration/02_worked_sequence.adoc[worked example]
  in the
  link:ch10_level1_orchestration/_chapter.adoc#level1-orchestration[Level-1 orchestration chapter]
  below. This describes a practised pattern for an external agent, not an
  integrated WFC product capability.

The link:../README.adoc[authority index] classifies these sources.

Today's commands are the installed, pip-installable, consumer-neutral
product Front Door (`wfc init`/`plan`/`request`/`prepare-checkout`/`run`/
`resume`/`status`/`audit`/`report`/`archive`), usable against an unrelated
consumer repository
without a WFC source checkout. The historical repo-local Harness
(`scripts/wfc_op.py`) that preceded it is retired, per the BP7 retirement
cutover plan. For WFC self-development, the repository `wfc run` is pointed
at happens to be a WFC Development source checkout; that is the
specialization where Operator and Developer roles coincide in one
maintainer, not the general consumer workflow.
