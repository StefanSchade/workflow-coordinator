This manual is *partial*: only orientation has handed over. Start with the
orientation below, then follow the reading path to the existing owners for
all other tasks. The remaining link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc[operational
reference] bodies and the dedicated contracts retain their standing;
orientation pointers at the old path preserve earlier reading links.

[[reading-path]]
== Reading path

. Read link:ch01_orientation/01_model.adoc[the mental model] and
  link:ch01_orientation/02_boundaries.adoc[execution boundaries] below.
. For Commission inputs, authority, authoring and review, use the
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_4_inputs_and_ownershiplifetime[inputs and ownership/lifetime],
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_6_normal_operator_workflow[normal workflow and Direct-v1 quickstart],
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_12_max_autonomous_hops[Hop safety ceiling], and
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_22_trusted_evidence_worker_completion_and_correction_routing[trusted acceptance and correction routing].
. For the self-development specialization, use the normative
  link:../self-development.adoc[self-development contract and Runtime
  promotion runbook], the
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_15_runtime_promotion[Runtime promotion overview], and the informative
  link:../operator_guide/ch08_self_development.adoc[guide chapter].
. For preparation through `PREPARED / NOT LAUNCHED`, use the normative
  link:../run-preparation.adoc[Run-preparation contract] and the current
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_7_prepare[Harness preparation reference];
  the link:../operator_guide/ch02_run_preparation.adoc[guide preparation chapter]
  is an informative reading aid.
. For launch, monitoring and stop interpretation, use
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_8_launch[LAUNCH],
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_9_status[STATUS], and
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_13_terminal_states_and_awaiting_human[terminal states].
. For evidence and archiving, use
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_10_archive[ARCHIVE],
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_11_semantic_ledger_curation[semantic ledger curation], and
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_16_evidence_architecture_current_state_vs_future_direction[evidence architecture].
. For a stopped Run, use the normative
  link:../run-recovery-and-salvage.adoc[recovery and salvage contract],
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_14_successor_runs[successor overview], and
  link:../successor_continuation_runbook_2026-10-10.adoc[successor-continuation runbook]
  §§3–4 for the current procedure; its worked case and captured experience
  are illustrative background.
. Consult the operational reference for
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_17_troubleshooting[troubleshooting], the
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_19_worked_example[worked example], and the
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_21_quick_reference_command_flow[command flow].

The link:../operator_guide/operator_guide.adoc[transitional Operator Guide]
retains its preparation/self-development includes and launch/archive/recovery
guidance. The link:../README.adoc[authority index] classifies these sources.

Today's commands run from a WFC Development source checkout
(`python3 scripts/wfc_op.py ...`). A pip-installable, consumer-neutral
Operator Harness and Front Door, usable against an unrelated repository
without that checkout, remains a product target, not an available path.
Self-development is the specialization where Operator and Developer roles
coincide in one maintainer; it is not the general consumer workflow.
