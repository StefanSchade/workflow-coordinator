This manual is *partial*: orientation, commissioning, running and evidence have handed over.
Start with those chapters below, then follow the reading path to the
existing owners for the remaining tasks. The remaining
link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc[operational reference] bodies and the
dedicated contracts retain their standing; handed-over
pointers at the old path preserve earlier reading links.

[[reading-path]]
== Reading path

. Read link:ch01_orientation/01_model.adoc[the mental model] and
  link:ch01_orientation/02_boundaries.adoc[execution boundaries] below.
. For Commission inputs, authority, authoring and review, read the
  link:ch02_commissioning/_chapter.adoc#commissioning[commissioning chapter]
  below. For trusted publication, evidence and acceptance, read
  link:ch04_evidence/01_acceptance.adoc#trusted-acceptance[trusted acceptance and correction routing].
. For the self-development specialization, use the normative
  link:../self-development.adoc[self-development contract and Runtime
  promotion runbook], the
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_15_runtime_promotion[Runtime promotion overview], and the informative
  link:../operator_guide/ch08_self_development.adoc[guide chapter].
. For preparation through `PREPARED / NOT LAUNCHED`, read the normative
  link:ch03_running/01_preparation.adoc[Run-preparation section], including
  its informative Harness command and self-development substrate distinction.
. For launch, monitoring and stop interpretation, read
  link:ch03_running/02_launch_status.adoc[launch, status and terminal states]
  in the link:ch03_running/_chapter.adoc#running[running chapter] below.
. For evidence and archiving, use
  link:ch04_evidence/02_archive.adoc#archive[ARCHIVE],
  link:ch04_evidence/02_archive.adoc#semantic-ledger[semantic ledger curation], and
  link:ch04_evidence/02_archive.adoc#evidence-architecture[evidence architecture]
  in the link:ch04_evidence/_chapter.adoc#evidence[evidence chapter] below.
. For a stopped Run, use the normative
  link:../run-recovery-and-salvage.adoc[recovery and salvage contract],
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_14_successor_runs[successor overview], and
  link:../successor_continuation_runbook_2026-10-10.adoc[successor-continuation runbook]
  §§3–4 for the current procedure; its worked case and captured experience
  are illustrative background.
. Consult the operational reference for
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_17_troubleshooting[troubleshooting], the
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_19_worked_example[worked example], and the
  link:../wfc_operator_manual.adoc#_21_quick_reference_command_flow[command flow].

The link:../operator_guide/operator_guide.adoc[transitional Operator Guide]
retains its self-development include, launch/archive navigation and recovery guidance.
The link:../README.adoc[authority index] classifies these sources.

Today's commands run from a WFC Development source checkout
(`python3 scripts/wfc_op.py ...`). A pip-installable, consumer-neutral
Operator Harness and Front Door, usable against an unrelated repository
without that checkout, remains a product target, not an available path.
Self-development is the specialization where Operator and Developer roles
coincide in one maintainer; it is not the general consumer workflow.
