== Maintainer decisions — 2026-11-18

=== Accepted decisions

* Beta 1 supports `simple@v1` and `robust@v1`. `direct@v1` is outside
  Beta-1 scope; its existing open questions and defects remain unchanged.
* The consumer names project-specific guidance files. They are frozen at
  Commission creation with traceable provenance and provided in all phases
  through the existing `repository_policy` component slot. Existing role
  components continue to determine Worker and Reviewer behavior. Guidance
  cannot expand scope, authority, permitted actions, routing or Finding
  authority. This extension alone does not require a new version of either
  supported program. Role- or phase-specific guidance remains a possible
  post-Beta-1 extension, outside the present minimum.

=== Requirements and preferences

* Preserve agent work that was not normally adopted as automatically as
  practical, particularly at `awaiting_human`, other stops or interruptions.
  Successful Runs whose commits were normally adopted need no additional
  candidate preservation. This is preservation, not full Run or provider
  session recovery.
* Prefer recording provider-reported token usage in the Beta-1 audit when
  feasible at reasonable cost. Missing values must remain unknown, not zero.
* Prefer a separate Git-based Evidence repository as the default, while
  retaining repository-local archiving as an option. This preference conflicts
  with the older repository-local preference in `DEC-WFC-023`; reconciliation
  remains open and that historical decision is not rewritten here.

=== Open design questions and qualification

Preservation mechanics and limits during Coordinator crashes remain to be
resolved. Token-usage implementation effort and its release-gate status are
open. Evidence configuration, protection boundaries, raw retention and the
sanitized audit projection also remain to be decided.

The Claude Code adapter is operationally proven in successful WFC Runs. The
positive live qualification for creating and writing a new authorized directory
through WFC candidate acceptance remains open for Beta 1; see
link:BUG-WFC-CLAUDE-ADAPTER-WRITE-PERMISSION-MISMATCH.adoc[the write-permission BUG].
The former Claude-specific preventive negative criterion is superseded.
Provider-independent preventive scope isolation, dispatch integration and
positive/negative qualification belong post Beta 1 to
link:FTR-WFC-EXECUTION-TRUST-BOUNDARIES.adoc[Execution Trust Boundaries].
Existing provider safeguards and WFC's candidate-scope checks remain in place;
the latter are not preventive isolation, which Beta 1 must not claim.

== Planning constraints and decisions still open

Business Commission and concrete execution bindings remain separate. A change
of model must not change the business Commission bundle; the actual bindings
must be frozen and visible in execution/audit evidence. One interactive
confirmation or `--yes` covers the authorized lifecycle. `wfc plan` runs no
project command; any baseline probe belongs after authorization. Prefer a
separate Git-backed Evidence repository with a sanitized audit projection.
Historical Commissions need intelligible provenance, not perpetual launch or
recompile compatibility with current APIs.

The third review's Linux host/subreaper, comprehensive quiescence machinery,
mandatory BP4a dependency and associated extra refusals are *superseded for
Beta 1*. Initially document Coordinator crashes and potentially surviving
provider processes as limitations. Process observations/questions remain in
link:../research/README.adoc#r2[R2]. Automatic preservation of unadopted work
is a requirement, with its mechanics and limits open; convenient continuation can follow shortly after the
release. The third review's full crash-preservation/lineage schema is not
therefore a mandatory foundation. Keep additions proportional to concrete
product goals and plausible failure cases.

Open decisions, using historical review labels only as navigation:

* R2 Q2 and Q7: guidance slot and Beta program catalogue are decided as
  recorded above; implementation details remain for BP2.
* R2 Q3 favors separate Evidence plus sanitized projection as a preference. Exact raw retention/defaults, projection fields and destination
  ergonomics still need bounded design. R2 Q5 preparation-contract amendment
  and Q6 cutover/tag timing remain open for BP7.
* R2 Q8: token usage as release gate or fast-follow remains unresolved.
  The October MUST-HAVE and later review recommendation are both provenance;
  no gate is silently changed here.
* R3 C1/C2/C3 settle the planning boundary for bindings, unattended execution
  and non-executing plan. R3 M1/M2 need a narrower preservation disposition
  before implementation; no automatic adoption of alternative B. M3's
  supervision platform choice is not a Beta-1 prerequisite. M4/M5 (candidate
  adoption and derived broad-review entry), X5 (no-change worker behavior)
  and M6 (old descriptor relationship) belong to later continuation design.
