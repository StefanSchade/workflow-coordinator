== Problem

A Level-1 operator session gets its operating knowledge from the shipped
Agent Skill (`src/workflow_coordinator/agent_skill/wfc/`) and the Operator
Manual. Across one long session, eighteen recorded friction entries traced
back to something those texts do not say, say in a superseded form, or say
wrongly. Two examples establish the shape:

* *A capability that exists and is invisible.* `wfc resume` covers only the
  mechanical durable-artifact anomaly class (`reducer.py:487-500`,
  `frontdoor/lifecycle.py:871-875`) and therefore correctly refused an
  operational `awaiting_human` stop. The productized answer channel for such
  a stop is `prepare-successor --authority-decision`
  (`operator.py:324-342`), eligible because an escalation of any kind --
  `operational` included -- freezes the semantic stop its gate requires
  (`reducer.py:1753-1759`, gate at `operator.py:1688`). The skill describes
  neither `prepare-successor` nor `export-salvage`, lists "Run a `wfc`
  subcommand this skill does not describe" under *Never* (`SKILL.md:131`) and
  warns that an unrecognized bare subcommand falls through to a legacy entry
  point (`SKILL.md:136-137`); the manual shows the command only as
  `python -m workflow_coordinator.operator prepare-successor`
  (`ch06_recovery/02_successors.adoc:130-131`). The operator could not reach
  a channel it was told not to look for.
* *A statement that is wrong.*
  `ch03_running/01_preparation.adoc:315-317` groups `--request` with
  `--guidance-path` and `--scope` as "repository-relative to
  `--repository`". `wfc run` reads `--request` as a plain filesystem path
  (`frontdoor/lifecycle.py:287`), and the skill already states this correctly
  (`references/commission.md:45`). Three arguments with three distinct path
  contracts -- `--scope`/`--guidance-path` repository-relative,
  `--config` repository-relative *and* committed (enforced at
  `frontdoor/verify.py:71`), `--request` a free filesystem path -- are
  documented as one. That conflation is the shape of friction entry 2, where
  a Consumer Config placed outside the repository was accepted by `wfc plan`
  and refused only after authorization, consuming the checkout.

== Direction

One Commission that writes the missing and corrected statements into the
Agent Skill references and Operator Manual chapters 2, 3, 6, 9 and 10. The
plan's link:beta2_operator_efficiency_plan.adoc#o1[O1 acceptance section]
enumerates every item with the friction entry it answers and the `path:line`
that makes it true; the load-bearing ones are the evidence-command rule (the
command must run the repository's own gates, with friction entry 18 as the
worked example of what a deterministic gate must prevent), the 900-second
evidence cap and its coupling to the prepared environment, the
`prepare-successor` channel above, the per-checkout environment recipe with
the shared-editable-install hazard, and the correction quoted above.

This record is also the *specification* for four later Front Door slices.
Four of its statements describe a workaround that those slices remove, so
they are written as *planned* beside the slice that retires each; see
link:beta2_operator_efficiency_plan.adoc#ownership[the ownership table].

== Open decisions

None of its own. Its content depends on no undecided question; the four
*planned* statements depend on the decisions of the slices that retire them
(link:beta2_operator_efficiency_plan.adoc#decisions[D1, D2 and D4]), and the
text stands unchanged whichever way those go -- it describes today's
behaviour either way.

One judgement, recorded rather than decided: the shipped texts reach a
consumer only through a Runtime promotion and `wfc skill install`, which are
maintainer actions. An accepted candidate therefore improves the repository's
texts immediately and the installed operator experience only on promotion.
