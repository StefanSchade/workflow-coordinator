== Core idea

A bounded decision-analysis task does not have the same shape as an
ordinary implementation task, or exactly the same shape as
link:FTR-WFC-INVESTIGATION-WORKFLOW.adoc[the investigation workflow] above.
A candidate choreography, recorded here only as illustrative, not as a
committed topology:

----
decision problem
    |
    v
alternatives
    |
    v
evaluation criteria
    |
    v
independent assessments (one per alternative, or per assessor)
    |
    v
counterarguments
    |
    v
synthesis
    |
    v
decision brief
----

This is, in essence, a structured multi-criteria utility analysis
("Nutzwertanalyse"), potentially with explicit criterion weighting and a
sensitivity analysis over those weights -- neither committed here, both
left as implementation-time questions for whenever this is actually
designed.

== Essential property

Dissent must be preservable rather than corrected away by a review loop.
`robust@v1`'s independent-review/correction cycle is built to converge on
one accepted candidate; a decision analysis instead wants to surface and
retain genuinely competing assessments and counterarguments as part of its
own output, not resolve them into a single agreed position before the
record is considered complete. A design that routes disagreement between
assessments through ordinary Finding correction risks silently discarding
exactly the disagreement the workflow exists to capture.

== Open questions this record deliberately does not resolve

* Whether this becomes a new workflow programme, or additional roles/
  phases/transitions within an existing one (including, possibly, the
  investigation workflow above) -- no preference is recorded, and no
  assumption is made that the two ideas share one programme.
* How multiple independent assessments/assessors are represented and kept
  distinct through the workflow, rather than merged prematurely.
* Whether and how weighting and sensitivity analysis are modeled, versus
  left to the synthesis step's own prose.
* What a durable artifact (the decision brief) requires structurally, and
  whether it must preserve the dissenting material itself, a pointer to
  it, or both.

== Non-goals (for this record)

This record does not itself: design a workflow programme or profile;
propose a schema, phase, or role; decide product scope; commit to a
specific multi-criteria decision-analysis method or weighting scheme; or
authorize any implementation. It exists to preserve the idea and its one
essential property (above) against loss, not to settle how it would be
built.
