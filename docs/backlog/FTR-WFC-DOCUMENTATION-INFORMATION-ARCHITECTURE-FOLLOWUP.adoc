== Context and intent

WFC began as internal tooling in spreadsheet-handling, was carved out using `git filter-repo`, then evolved rapidly with functionality and architecture taking precedence over exhaustive historical reference and governance consolidation. Today's documents reflect multiple repository and development generations. Unresolved historical links are expected in that context: their existence is neither a surprise quality crisis nor proof of K1 failure.

Future work may improve the clarity, maintainability and navigation of active documentation and redesign information architecture when warranted. Preserve the substance and provenance of historical statements; do not cosmetically rewrite historical originals to force current paths to resolve. Historical compatibility is a cost, not an end in itself. In a separately authorized design, the entry paths, headings and anchors deliberately preserved by M1/M2 may be reconsidered, including deliberate breaks where the benefit warrants them; assess actual consumers and consequences rather than requiring universal backward compatibility. No particular path break is decided here.

== Classification to use in later planning

. *Active and actually broken:* correct when it impairs a current reading or operating path.
. *Historical break after carve-out or archiving:* normally tolerate; identify diagnostically where useful, without rewriting original history.
. *Historical source particularly relevant to current work:* consider a targeted modern navigation pointer or reference while leaving the original intact.
. *Illustrative path:* distinguish examples from productive links; do not count them as broken functional navigation.

K1's unresolved-link list consists of diagnostic leads, not pre-classified defects; assess source context before making a claim. A zero-unresolved-historical-links target is explicitly not an acceptance criterion. A future package should demonstrate that relevant active paths are usable and consistent, retain evidence and provenance, and explain deliberate trade-offs and compatibility decisions. Avoid blanket migration, historical repair quotas or full backwards compatibility as a precondition.
