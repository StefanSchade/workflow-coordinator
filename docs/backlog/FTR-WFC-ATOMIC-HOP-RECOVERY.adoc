= FTR-WFC-ATOMIC-HOP-RECOVERY -- Atomic Hop Interruption and Recovery Model

Type:: future technical record
Status:: proposed / backlog / architecture-convergence draft
Priority:: small recovery MVP; later work is evidence-gated
Date:: 2026-09-24
Recorded now:: Current fail-closed interruption handling is sound. A narrow
operator reconcile/resume path appears useful; same-Hop retry is an unproven
optimization, not the next inevitable implementation.

== Decision summary

Current conservative behavior remains authoritative. Every Hop appended to
`run.hops` is charged against `max_autonomous_hops`. A validated, correlated
candidate is charged regardless of semantic Outcome, including
`Outcome.FAILED`; an invocation whose acceptance cannot be disproved is charged
as an uncertain Hop and requires reconciliation. Only a future
machine-proven, candidate-free, fully quiescent transient Attempt may be
discarded without producing a Hop. Only a Hop with a validated, correlated
candidate may reach `reduce_result`.

The cheapest useful near-term enhancement is an explicit operator/CLI path to
reconcile and resume the *same Run*. The repository already contains the main
library seams; the one-shot operator does not wire them. This preserves
Run-local Findings, phase, pinned governing truth, and charged-Hop history. It
retains the conservative charge and requires explicit human action; it does not
replay the interrupted invocation.

Attempt observability is independently useful and should evolve the existing
execution-evidence and dispatch-marker surfaces. Bounded same-Hop retry is a
later optimization, opened only by operational evidence that its saved Hop and
human action are materially valuable and only after the relevant execution
containment gap is addressed. Persistent provider-session continuation is not
part of this plan. Predecessor scratch and generalized process-loss recovery
remain deferred. A fresh successor Run remains correct when governing,
Runtime, contract, or other system truth changes.

This FTR is architecture-convergence input, not implementation authority. It
does not approve an implementation or supersede current semantics.

== Empirical basis and scope

Seven WFC-on-WFC dogfood attempts are recorded. None records an operational
`uncertain_dispatch` or transient provider interruption. Observed failures so
far were semantic, governance, or implementation defects. The frequency and
cost of quota or other transient interruption loss are therefore unmeasured.

The minimum useful recovery capability and an optimized retry mechanism are
different decisions:

* minimum useful capability: explicit reconcile/resume of an existing Run;
* useful observability: a durable record for each invocation Attempt and any
  stable structured transient facts;
* later optimization: bounded same-Hop retry, if measured need justifies it;
* deliberately deferred: predecessor scratch and generalized process-loss
  recovery.

R1 and any future R2 initially target an intact Coordinator process. The
reconcile/resume entrypoint is a separate, narrow process-level re-entry
capability. This FTR does not require same-Hop retry to survive Coordinator
process loss.

== Precise vocabulary and authority

Run:: One work episode under pinned WorkItem, profile, policy, governing inputs,
repository identity, and baseline.

Charged Hop:: A Hop appended to `run.hops`. Every charged Hop consumes one unit
of `max_autonomous_hops`, including an uncertain Hop.

Candidate-bearing Hop:: A charged Hop whose invocation produced a validated,
correlated candidate. Its semantic Outcome may be successful or failed. It is
the only kind of Hop that may reach `reduce_result`.

Uncertain Hop:: A charged Hop synthesized because invocation acceptance cannot
be disproved. It has no reduction authority and moves the Run to
`reconcile_required`.

Attempt:: A subordinate durable execution record for one invocation intended
to produce a pending Hop result. `Run -> Hop -> Attempt` is useful trace
vocabulary; Attempt is not a new independent lifecycle tier or state machine.

Discarded Attempt:: A future R2 classification limited to a machine-proven
transient, candidate-free, fully quiescent Attempt at an unchanged eligible
boundary. It produces no Hop and has no reduction authority.

Reconcile/resume:: Explicit human authorization to continue the same Run after
current reality has been validated. The already charged uncertain Hop remains
in history; the next invocation uses the next Hop.

Fresh successor Run:: A new episode that admits current truth. It does not
implicitly inherit Run-local Findings.

The terms adapter acceptance, candidate validation, invocation status, Hop
charging, semantic Outcome, and reducer/route validation are distinct. This FTR
does not use a bare word such as "accepted" to stand for more than one of them.

== Charging and reduction rules

The following rules are the load-bearing contract:

. A charged Hop is any Hop appended to `run.hops`; every charged Hop consumes
  `max_autonomous_hops`.
. An Attempt with a validated, correlated candidate produces a charged Hop
  irrespective of semantic Outcome, explicitly including `Outcome.FAILED`.
. An invocation whose acceptance cannot be disproved produces a charged
  uncertain Hop and `reconcile_required`.
. Only a machine-proven transient, candidate-free, fully quiescent discarded
  Attempt may eventually produce no Hop.
. Charging and reduction authority are separate: only a Hop carrying a
  validated, correlated candidate may reach `reduce_result`.

A semantic failure must not become retryable merely because another model
invocation might produce a better answer. Malformed or uncorrelated output,
reducer preemption, unauthorized action, dirty repository state, changed
governing input, or an invariant failure is likewise not reclassified as a
transient interruption.

These rules preserve current conservative behavior unless a separately
approved R2 narrows it for the proven discarded-Attempt subset. Such an R2
would explicitly create a machine-proven exception to the normative P5 rules:

* `WFC-AUTO-02`: post-dispatch invocations are charged, and acceptance that
  cannot be disproved is charged and never replayed;
* `WFC-AUTO-03`: V1 has no hidden retry; and
* `WFC-AUTO-05`: resume/recovery requires explicit maintainer authority and
  used Hops never reset.

This FTR does not supersede those rules. They remain normative until a separate
approved implementation change says otherwise.

== Current repository facts

=== Charge and interruption behavior

`model.record_hop()` appends a Hop and increments `hop_used`.
`vertical.run_one_hop()` validates candidate correlation, records the Hop, and
then calls the reducer. Consequently `Outcome.FAILED` is charged before the
reducer stops the Run.

After a dispatch marker exists, candidate absence, malformed or uncorrelated
output, and unclassified exceptions enter
`persistence.recover_uncertain_dispatch()`. It records an
`uncertain_dispatch` Hop with
`invocation_status=acceptance_not_disproved`, charges one Hop, requires
reconciliation, and retains the marker. Unchanged HEAD alone does not make the
invocation replayable. Proven pre-execution failures clear the marker and
consume no Hop.

Run snapshots, markers, checkpoints, and individual evidence files are
atomically published, but they and Git are not one transaction. Repository
mutation is observed, not rolled back.

=== Existing reconcile/resume seams

Three existing seams are directly relevant:

* `serialization.load_run()` reconstructs the durable Run from `run.json`;
* `persistence.load_dispatch_marker()` reconstructs correlated dispatch facts;
* `reducer.resume_after_reconcile()` changes the same
  `RECONCILE_REQUIRED` Run to `READY` only when the phase still resolves,
  anomalies are absent, current HEAD equals the reconciled Run HEAD, and used
  Hops do not exceed the limit.

Uncertain-dispatch recovery is idempotent when the marker's Hop is already in
history. The external state also records the checkout association and an
operator plan with input paths, pinned revision, governing digests, executable,
timeout, and evidence plan. Together, these show that a narrow entrypoint can
reload and validate the same episode without first introducing R1 evidence
changes.

The missing production seam is the operator: `_preflight()` rejects any
existing state root with "one-shot operator roots are never overwritten" and
`main()` always creates a new Run. A future entrypoint must be explicitly
separate from new-Run preflight, must validate the registered checkout,
clean/resolved repository state and pinned governing identity, must establish
that the predecessor process is no longer live, and must invoke reconciliation
and resume deliberately. It must persist the reconciled/resumed Run and
checkpoint before retiring the reconciled predecessor marker or permitting any
later dispatch. It must not infer replay authority from the marker.

This entrypoint has no technical dependency on richer Attempt evidence. It can
be implemented independently of R1, subject to its own focused design and
tests, and is substantially smaller than R2.

=== Existing Attempt and correlation surfaces

The minimal trace substrate already exists:

* `Invocation` carries `run_id`, `hop_id`, and `invocation_id`;
* `DispatchMarker` persists those correlated facts plus sequence, base HEAD,
  and reserved budget;
* `execution-evidence.json` is schema version 2 and already contains an
  `attempts` array with execution and repository facts.

There are three concrete gaps:

. evidence rows are derived from `run.hops[-1]`, not directly from the
  invocation;
. `recover_uncertain(None)` can produce no evidence row; and
. there is no Attempt ordinal or structured disposition.

R1 should extend these existing surfaces, not create a second persistence
system or an independent Attempt state machine. An `invocation_id` reference
on each candidate-bearing `HopSummary` is a reasonable direct correlation from
the charged Hop to its producing Attempt.

=== Current Codex execution binding

The current adapter argv includes, approximately and without claiming more CLI
syntax than source establishes, `--ask-for-approval never`, `exec`,
`--ephemeral`, `--json`, `--sandbox workspace-write`, `--model`, the model
reasoning-effort configuration, `--cd <repository>`, and output-schema and
last-message paths. The prompt is supplied on stdin.

The adapter uses `--ephemeral`. Local Codex help describes `codex exec resume`
as resuming recorded sessions; an ephemeral invocation deliberately does not
persist that session state. Provider-session continuation would therefore
require changing this trust and persistence choice. It is a non-goal for the
recovery MVP, and this project will not introduce persistent provider sessions
merely to address quota interruption.

The narrow remaining provider question belongs in R1: does Codex `--json`
JSONL and/or process exit status expose a stable structured quota/transient
signal that can be retained without parsing human diagnostic prose? Matching
text such as "5 hour limit" is prohibited.

== Three recovery mechanisms

[cols="1,2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Concern |Same-Hop retry |Reconcile + resume same Run |Fresh successor Run

|Identity and history
|Same Run and pending Hop; predecessor Attempt is subordinate trace.
|Same Run; uncertain Hop remains charged; next Hop is used.
|New Run and newly admitted truth.

|Findings, phase, governing truth
|Preserved from the pre-Attempt Run boundary.
|Preserved in the loaded Run: Run-local Findings, phase, and pinned governing
identity remain intact.
|No implicit Finding transfer. Phase and truth are selected and admitted
afresh.

|Mechanism cost
|Needs reliable transient classification, Attempt allowance, quiescence and
containment proof, and safe marker transition.
|Needs a narrow existing-state operator path around existing load/reducer
seams plus explicit reconciliation.
|Closest to today's one-shot operator workflow.

|Residual benefit
|Avoids charging the interrupted Attempt as a Hop, avoids one explicit
reconciliation/human action, and gives a cleaner subordinate Attempt trace.
|Preserves useful Run context cheaply while retaining the conservative charge
and human action.
|Correct whenever governing or system truth changed; often cheap when little
Run-local context exists.
|===

The residual value of same-Hop retry is useful but modest. It must not be
described as necessary merely to preserve Findings.

Case A -- H001 review creates F001; H002 correction is interrupted::
Reconcile and resume the same Run is a valid cheap MVP path. H002 remains a
charged uncertain Hop; resumption continues with the next Hop while preserving
F001, phase, and pinned truth. R2 would save one Hop and one human action.

Case B -- Contract, Runtime, or governing truth is corrected::
Use a fresh successor Run. The old Run must not adopt changed governing truth,
and Findings are not implicitly transferred.

Case C -- clean initial review is interrupted before a useful result::
A fresh successor Run may already be cheap enough because there is no valuable
Run-local result to preserve. Explicit same-Run resume remains possible when
the operator values identity continuity, but it does not make R2 necessary.

== Future R2 eligibility: facts and residual risks

Future same-Hop retry must use a conjunctive, machine-derived subset. It must
not claim that Git cleanliness proves absence of side effects.

=== A. Machine-derived repository and recovery predicates

At minimum, the inner WFC must establish:

* the exact same Run, WorkItem, pending Hop/sequence, phase, pre-Attempt Run
  snapshot, checkout/repository identity, and base HEAD;
* exact base HEAD still current; tracked and untracked non-ignored paths clean;
  submodules resolved;
* unchanged profile, policy, governing-input, task/component, operating
  contract, authorized scope/action, evidence-plan, and relevant execution
  binding identities;
* no validated correlated candidate and no applied trusted commit, evidence,
  Finding delta, route, or other authoritative result from the Attempt;
* a stable structured transient classification rather than prose inference;
* predecessor process termination and absence of a concurrent Attempt, but
  only to the degree the execution boundary can actually observe them; and
* remaining durable Attempt/retry allowance and applicable deadline/backoff.

=== B. Residual risks outside current observability

Current primitives do not prove "no unresolved side effect." Uncovered risks
include:

* ignored repository paths;
* writes outside Git or outside the repository;
* escaped descendant processes;
* network effects and external service mutation;
* credentials and their use; and
* mutable caches.

This is a pre-existing trust-boundary gap across ordinary Hops, not a new class
of retry vulnerability. Retry crosses that boundary again, however, and cannot
pretend repository cleanliness closes it. R2 therefore depends on the relevant
technical-containment direction in
`FTR-WFC-EXECUTION-TRUST-BOUNDARIES.adoc`, unless a later review identifies a
narrow execution class for which sufficient isolation is machine-provable.
Containment is not designed or implemented here.

== Recovery facts exposed by the inner WFC

The inner WFC may publish a mechanically derived finite set named, for example,
`permitted_recovery_actions`. The set may be empty. Each member must follow
only from predicates the inner WFC can establish, alongside the underlying
identity, candidate, process, repository, governing, budget, and reason facts.

This follows the existing `permitted_route_keys` and
`next_permitted_route_keys` precedent: the inner system derives bounded
permission, not policy choice. It must not select among retry, same-Run resume,
fresh successor, or human handling, and it must not initiate a successor Run.
A future outer loop or human applies policy, budget, and authority to choose an
action. Any example mapping from a recovery class to an action is therefore
non-normative outer-loop behavior, not a reducer decision table.

== Budget and durable trace semantics

The semantic Hop budget and a possible technical Attempt/recovery allowance
are distinct:

* every charged Hop consumes `max_autonomous_hops`;
* a validated correlated candidate consumes one semantic Hop irrespective of
  its Outcome, including `Outcome.FAILED`; this is unchanged from current
  semantics;
* a charged uncertain Hop also consumes that budget;
* only a future proven discarded Attempt consumes no Hop; and
* no used Hop is decremented or reset.

If R2 is ever implemented, every retry gets a new invocation identity and an
Attempt ordinal under the unchanged pending Hop. Its finite Attempt/retry
allowance must be durable across Coordinator crash so an in-memory counter
cannot reset into an unbounded loop. Allowance exhaustion falls back to the
conservative charged-Hop and reconcile behavior; it must not silently widen
retry or invent a permissive terminal state. This FTR deliberately does not
choose whether configuration belongs in policy, WorkItem, adapter settings, or
another governed authority surface.

Invocation correlation already prevents stale predecessor result content from
being silently consumed as the successor result: validation checks
`run_id`/`hop_id`/`invocation_id`. Attempt-specific result paths are therefore
primarily a liveness, observability, and overwrite-risk improvement, not the
core safety precondition.

For R2 marker transition, one ordering rule is mandatory: there must be no
window in which no durable marker covers a possibly-live Attempt. If successor
and predecessor marker coverage overlap, the successor marker becomes durable
*before* predecessor coverage is retired. This is a bounded ordering
requirement, not a proposal for a general transaction system.

== Deferred predecessor scratch

Predecessor scratch is optional salvage, never resume. It remains deferred
until operational evidence shows expensive rediscovery is material.

If later introduced, it is never authoritative, accepted evidence, Finding
state or disposition, governing input, restored provider-session state, or a
source of authority. It is never automatically executed, does not widen
authority merely to expose it, and remains untrusted material whose facts must
be independently verified. A future implementation depends on suitable
prompt/context trust labels and technical containment, neither of which this
FTR supplies. A bounded per-Attempt directory and Coordinator-authored manifest
may eventually be useful illustrations; directory taxonomy, exact manifest
schema, eligibility details, and prompt wording are intentionally undecided.

== Staged direction

=== Architecture observation sequence

R1 -- Attempt observation and structured transient evidence::
Evolve the existing execution-evidence `attempts` array and marker surfaces.
Derive rows from invocations, publish a row wherever possible including
uncertain paths, add Attempt ordinal and a finite structured disposition such
as `not_started | candidate_validated | discarded | uncertain`, and retain the
bounded JSONL/exit-status facts needed to assess a stable quota signal. Derive
`permitted_recovery_actions`. Make no recovery semantic change.

R1.5 -- explicit reconcile/resume operator entrypoint::
Wire existing Run and marker loading, checkout/governing validation,
uncertain-dispatch reconciliation, and `resume_after_reconcile` for the same
Run. Preserve Findings, phase, governing identity, and charged history. Require
explicit human/operator action, retain the already charged uncertain Hop, and
perform no automatic replay.

R2 -- bounded same-Hop retry::
Open only if R1 or equivalent operational evidence records real transient,
candidate-free, quiescent Attempts and shows that their charged-Hop plus
explicit-reconciliation cost is material enough to justify R2. Limit it to the
machine-proven subset, depend on relevant containment, and retain conservative
charging for every ambiguous or ineligible case.

R3 -- predecessor scratch and generalized process-loss recovery::
Remain deferred and evidence-driven. Neither blocks a practical recovery MVP.

=== Smallest useful implementation slice

The observation sequence puts R1 first because it supplies evidence for R2.
The implementation sequence need not. R1.5 is independent of R1 and appears
substantially smaller. The practical MVP may implement R1.5 first, stop there,
and return project effort to spreadsheet-handling/DMC work. R1 may proceed
independently when its observability value warrants it. R2 is not presumed to
follow.

== Non-goals

This FTR does not authorize or require:

* arbitrary mid-Hop checkpointing or resume;
* hidden reasoning serialization or restoration;
* automatic repository reset, clean, or rollback;
* retry of semantic failure or ambiguous/mutating execution;
* implicit Finding transfer between Runs;
* changing an existing Run's admitted governing truth;
* a generic outer loop or watchdog policy engine;
* elevated-privilege work;
* provider-session persistence or provider-session continuation;
* scratch implementation now;
* generalized process-restart recovery for same-Hop retry; or
* implementation approval through this architecture record.

== Remaining evidence questions

* Does Codex JSONL or exit status provide a stable structured transient/quota
  fact without diagnostic-prose matching?
* What narrower execution class or containment work can make the R2 residual
  risk acceptable?
* If measured need opens R2, where should durable Attempt allowances and
  deadlines be governed?

Until those questions are answered by evidence and a separately reviewed
change, explicit reconcile/resume and fresh successor Runs are the practical
recovery mechanisms, and current charging semantics remain authoritative.
