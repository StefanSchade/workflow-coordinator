== 1. Problem, goal and concrete deliverable

The operator workflow is distributed across one 1,358-line manual, a
352-line transitional guide and three contracts plus a successor runbook.
This series plans a later modular manual, not its content. This Commission
creates only this charter, ten numbered work-package FTRs and the compact
manifest entry. No manual has been prototyped, written, moved or stubbed.
A missing later implementation is not a defect of this planning deliverable.

A technically competent reader without WFC history must eventually learn
repositories and authority; Commission authoring/validation; prepare,
launch and monitoring; evidence, acceptance and archive; failure diagnosis,
recovery and successors; and self-development. Existing normative contracts
retain their meaning. The final entry owns navigation, never a replacement
source of runtime authority.

single series card under “Planned, not yet ready” because the series is
conditional and not yet commissioned; its card identifies FTR01 as the
only initially eligible package. Readiness is not execution authority.

The cuts follow complete operator concepts, not arbitrary file counts:
orientation; Commission design; deterministic execution; trusted acceptance
and evidence; self-development; normative recovery; the long informative

new end-to-end walkthrough. FTR07 alone handles the 658-line runbook;

provider experiment or repository test authoring is bundled with migration.

not artificial document length targets. Each package includes its own
rendering and reference checks and must be a complete first Worker candidate.

each `ch*/_chapter.adoc` includes its stand-alone section files with
leveloffset +1. Eight chapter directories, one entry, eight wrappers and
nineteen section files yield 28 manual files after FTR10.
The exact leaf plans are in the numbered FTRs, not permission to create
them during planning.

`../core/docs/developer_guide/developer_guide.adoc` and

Wrappers and section files follow the local conventions' §1.7: a guarded
standalone title and metadata (`ifndef::assembled[]`), blank line after
title/attributes, one `=` title when read alone, content rooted at `==`.
The entry sets assembled before including wrappers; a wrapper also sets
assembled for its included sections after its own standalone header, so
included section metadata is suppressed both when the wrapper is opened
alone and when the entry is assembled. Wrappers contain navigation/heading
and includes, not a competing procedural body. Apply leveloffset in both
include layers and verify rendered heading levels. Choose explicit stable
anchors where old fragments or chapter-level cross-references need them;
validate IDs in standalone and assembled output, not just file existence.

The entry and wrappers are informative. Normative section files explicitly
claim exactly the preparation, self-development and recovery facts migrated
from their normative owners. A mixed chapter does not dilute those sections
into informative summaries. Historical case material is clearly labelled
illustration, not a migration-history artifact or current instruction.

=== Allocation table: interpretation

One row per actual source heading, identified by source path, original
line and exact heading text. OM includes every H2/H3; other documents
include every section depth. Headings inside literal examples are excluded.
A parent row assigns only its own introductory body, not its separately
listed child sections. Document titles/preambles/header metadata travel
with that document's first content allocation; OM's preamble is adapted by
FTR01 to the partial-handover rule, not copied as a second authority claim.
Guide chapter preambles travel with their respective full folds. The
successor preamble travels to its current procedure. Router and backlog
convention have no sections and receive an explicit whole-source row.

“Stays” is an explicit disposition with a reason, not unassigned work.
Navigation rows stay canonical at their existing paths; later packages
update those same rows as sources hand over, without a second allocation
of their substance. Duplicate source headings may converge on one target;
within that target keep one complete explanation and record the exact
merge in the future result. Never delete a warning or example as a mere
heading duplicate. No manual allocation table survives in product prose.
