== 1. Executive verdict

*ACCEPT WITH MATERIAL REVISIONS.*

The central boundary survives. Commissioning is a real concern, but the
repository does not support one trust-bearing service between Level 1 and
Level 0. Semantic authoring and deterministic projection belong on the
requesting side; admission-time derivation of capability, information-flow
eligibility, and mandatory assurance belongs inside trusted Level 0. A
component that both requests and admits those facts would terminate an
escalation inside its beneficiary and would conflict with the accepted
product boundary.

No BLOCKING defect was found in that split. Five MATERIAL corrections are
required before the concept should be treated as the preferred architecture
hypothesis:

. `CommissionIntent` is called canonical without a rule binding it to the
  independently authority-bearing projection.
. The concept mistakes and freezes the current supervised Git workflow as the
  authority root, although current authority requires a separate maintainer act
  and accepted architecture permits other protected authority sources.
. A standing assessment Commission does not resolve the recursion until its
  per-task input, readable context, and provenance envelope are specified.
. Model selection omits the possibility that trusted assurance policy imposes
  a minimum model-capability class, not only security/provider compatibility.
. The reported profile-corpus reduction is factually incomplete and the
  proposed entry-offset grammar omits current gate-entry continuation shapes.

These are finite amendments. They do not justify a Commissioning Service, move
project semantics into Level 0, or require redesign of the WFC product.

Finding count:: BLOCKING 0; MATERIAL 5.

== 3. Boundary reconstruction

The repository's accepted architecture requires the following boundary.

. *Level 1 owns project semantics.* Goal custody, task selection, task type,
  semantic expertise, decomposition, architecture disposition, successor
  planning, lifecycle interpretation, and closure remain above WFC's trusted
  core.
. *Level 1 is not an authority.* It may propose, request, select within an
  already protected envelope, and consume results. It may not grant itself
  capability, visibility, scope, topology, assurance reduction, or governing
  provenance.
. *Level 0 owns authority-moving mechanics.* Admission, trusted effects,
  evidence, fail-closed reduction, capability/security derivation, mandatory
  assurance derivation, and concrete execution-envelope enforcement belong in
  trusted Level 0. Most derivation/containment machinery is future direction,
  not current implementation.
. *No escalation terminates in its beneficiary.* Governing authority must be
  rooted outside and fixed before the requester can influence it. Agent output
  does not become authority by being committed, copied, referenced, rendered,
  or passed through a compiler.
. *Topology is requested, not supplied.* A requesting component may select
  among pre-authorized patterns; trusted policy/admission must reject a profile
  that falls below security or assurance floors.
. *Derivation is monotone.* Trusted derivation may narrow capability and raise
  assurance. It may not widen authority. Request-side additions may strengthen
  assurance or narrow the request but cannot remove a trusted minimum.
. *Information eligibility is pre-disclosure.* A later review cannot undo
  disclosure. Ingress, egress, provider/execution-boundary compatibility, and
  declassification must therefore be resolved before protected context is
  exposed.
. *Current Git practice is procedural.* Today an independently trusted
  maintainer reviews/approves the exact committed governing bundle and invokes
  WFC against its pinned commit. Git transports, exposes, pins, and audits that
  bundle. Repository state alone is not mechanical proof of authorization.
. *The future authority source is open.* A separately protected authority
  source may pre-authorize a bounded family of work. A human commit per
  WorkItem and hand-authored governing files are not accepted permanent
  invariants.
. *Level 1 remains open-ended.* Commission authoring is one Level-1-facing
  concern, not the definition of Level 1.

Against that reconstruction, the concept places the main split correctly. A
deterministic compiler is best understood as authority-free tooling or a
library used by the requesting side, not as an independently trust-bearing
runtime service. It has no independent authority, provider need, durable state
ownership, deployment boundary, or lifecycle that would justify a new system
layer. Exact packaging remains open; trust placement does not.

== 4. Findings

=== F-M1 -- CommissionIntent canonicality lacks a binding to execution authority

Severity:: MATERIAL

Claim under review::
The concept calls `CommissionIntent` the "canonical semantic truth" and calls
the four Commission-specific files its generated projection, while also keeping
those files independently inspectable, editable, committed, pinned, and the
only authority Level 0 admits.

Evidence::
Concept sections 5 and 12 say the renderer produces an ordinary dirty worktree,
`CommissionIntent` is canonical, and the generated files remain admitted
authority. Level 0 currently has no `CommissionIntent` input, digest, provenance
link, or render-equivalence check. `operator._preflight()` reads WorkItem,
Profile, Policy, Components, and Task from `expected_head`; its `governing` map
contains those texts and selected components only. The product-boundary
invariant says a convenience projection must not become an independent
authority source. The concept does not say what happens if a maintainer edits a
rendered governing file before commit, or if the intent and committed projection
later diverge.

Why it matters::
Without a mechanically checked render identity, two different truths can exist:
the intent says what was authored, while the admitted files say what executes.
For current Level 0, the files necessarily win. Calling the intent canonical
anyway makes the compiler a fifth parallel source rather than a replacement for
duplicated authorship, and can mislead later tooling into trusting stale intent
over admitted authority. This is the concept's strongest architectural
counterargument and is not answered by demonstrating that duplication exists.

Required disposition::
State the split precisely: in the minimal/current-compatible future,
`CommissionIntent` is at most the canonical *authoring source for a render
transaction*, while the exact committed and admitted governing inputs are the
canonical execution authority. Define whether any post-render edit invalidates
the intent/projection relationship and requires rerendering. Do not promote
`CommissionIntent` to persistent or execution canonicality unless a later design
mechanically binds the admitted projection to the exact intent and protected
authority provenance.

=== F-M2 -- The concept misidentifies and freezes the current authorizing act

Severity:: MATERIAL

Claim under review::
The concept repeatedly states `commit = the authorizing act`, says the
maintainer's commit is the authorizing act "exactly as today", and requires
hand-authored Commissions to remain admissible forever.

Evidence::
The current authority index states that committed governance alone neither
starts nor authorizes a Run and that each real Run additionally requires
explicit maintainer authorization. Product-boundary section 11.6 states that
the independently trusted operator's separate act, not repository state,
carries today's authority; current admission cannot distinguish a
maintainer-prepared bundle from agent-authored governance. The same accepted
section explicitly rejects one human act per WorkItem as an invariant and
permits a separately protected authority source to pre-authorize a bounded
family. The operator manual describes current practice as reviewed/committed
Commission inputs followed by a separate PREPARE/LAUNCH decision. `DEC-WFC-010`
establishes Git as the Project Memory review boundary; it does not establish Git
commit as the universal, permanent execution-authority root for Commissions.

Why it matters::
The shorthand can launder generated or agent-authored governance by implying
that a commit alone creates authority, the exact cross-Run provenance failure
the accepted product boundary leaves open. The "forever" rule also converts a
useful current safety valve into unsupported architecture and could block a
future mechanically protected authority source or generated-only authoring
path even when Level 0 remains correctly coupled only to admitted inputs.

Required disposition::
Label the present flow as *current supervised operating practice*: an
independently trusted maintainer/operator reviews or approves the exact bundle,
records it in Git, and explicitly launches against the pinned commit. Describe
Git as current review/transport/audit and admission anchor, not as the abstract
authority model. Replace "forever" with the invariant that Level 0 must not
depend on one convenience authoring tool and that any exclusive future path
must preserve a separately protected authority source, inspectable admitted
projection, and fail-closed provenance.

=== F-M3 -- A standing TaskAssessment fixture does not resolve the recursion

Severity:: MATERIAL

Claim under review::
The concept says the assessment recursion "dissolves" with one standing,
pre-authorized, read-only Commission per repository and calls that shape
near-expressible today using `authorized_scope: []`, no `edit`, no `commit`, and
no durable artifact.

Evidence::
Today the concrete Task is one of the five committed and pinned governing
inputs. A standing Commission therefore has a fixed task unless a new dynamic
input surface is added. `authorized_scope` bounds mutation; it does not bound
what the current provider process can read, and current Project Memory records
that WFC has no Coordinator-owned or verified visibility/containment boundary.
The concept does not specify how the new task enters the standing Run, what
task inputs are admissible, what repository/context the assessment may read,
which provider may receive it, or what provenance binds the advice to the
assessed input. A generic standing model channel with repository visibility is
therefore not made narrow merely by having no edit/commit authority.

Why it matters::
The recursion is shifted from "generate a Commission" to "authorize a dynamic
task and read envelope" rather than resolved. Treating the fixture as solved
could create an over-broad, long-lived agent channel for protected repository
content or lead implementation to use mutation scope as if it were read
containment. The assessment's advisory output type correctly grants no
authority, but that does not authorize its inputs or disclosures.

Required disposition::
Keep standing assessment as an open option. Before calling it sufficient,
define a pre-authorized input envelope for the concrete task, the admissible
read/context envelope, provider/execution compatibility, ingress/egress rules,
and provenance of both input and advice. State explicitly that assessment may
recommend but never grant scope, capability, information access, architecture
authority, or provider eligibility. Until such an envelope exists, protected
assessment remains ordinary separately admitted Level-0 work or an above-L0
untrusted advisory call limited to unprotected input.

=== F-M4 -- Model eligibility lacks a trusted minimum capability floor

Severity:: MATERIAL

Claim under review::
The model-selection split puts information-flow and privilege compatibility
below the boundary but puts quality -- including the proposition that an
independent architecture review deserves a stronger model -- wholly above it
as requester preference.

Evidence::
The accepted product boundary assigns mandatory assurance floors to trusted
Level 0 and leaves execution-class resolver ownership open. The role/persona/
binding FTR makes bindings authority-neutral only in the present MVP and
reserves future one-directional eligibility checks. The concept correctly says
trusted derivation enumerates an eligible set, but its stated reasons for
trusted eligibility are security/information compatibility and privilege; it
does not say that trusted policy may impose a minimum model capability or
assurance class for work whose mandatory control is a meaningful independent,
architecture, or security review. A request-side preference can therefore be
omitted or set below the assurance the trusted floor intends to obtain while
remaining security-compatible.

Why it matters::
Provider security compatibility and model capability are different predicates.
If a mandatory review may be performed by any security-eligible binding solely
because quality is requester-owned, Level 0 can enforce the presence of a gate
without enforcing the minimum competence class that makes the gate an assurance
control. That weakens the monotonic-floor thesis the concept otherwise states
correctly.

Required disposition::
Extend the trusted eligible-set formulation without inventing a score or
router: trusted policy may derive both the minimum eligible security/execution
boundary and a minimum provider/model capability or assurance class where the
accepted work class requires one. Request-side cost, latency, and quality
preferences choose only within that eligible set and may request a stronger
class, never a weaker one. Leave the concrete classification and resolver
mechanism open.

=== F-M5 -- The measured profile corpus and proposed entry grammar are incomplete

Severity:: MATERIAL

Claim under review::
The concept reports 36 profiles, 124 phases, 11 phase-name sets, and four
WORK/GATE structures, then presents one reviewed-work base plus a continuation
entry at the correction phase as a minimal grammar consistent with the corpus.

Evidence::
The file and phase totals are correct, as are all component-selector counts.
The topology summary is not. Independent parsing of the exact corpus at the
concept's parent/current commit yields 12 phase-name sets and five raw
WORK/GATE signatures with counts 20/7/5/2/2. Four profiles begin at a gate:
two are `GATE -> WORK -> GATE`, and two are `GATE -> WORK`; their WorkItems
enter at `review`, `independent_review`, or `rereview`. The concept instead
reports nine `WORK -> GATE` continuations and two `WORK -> GATE -> GATE`
profiles, a signature absent from the current corpus. Its minimal grammar names
only entry at correction.

Why it matters::
The quantitative corpus is presented as the decisive proof and as the source
for implementation step 4. A renderer built from the stated grammar can omit
four current, authority-bearing gate-entry continuation profiles or silently
reinterpret their entry semantics. This does not defeat composition -- the
four shapes may still reduce to the same reviewed-work graph with additional
entry offsets -- but that reduction has not been demonstrated by the reported
measurement.

Required disposition::
Correct the corpus measurement and distinguish raw YAML phase order, reachable
route graph, and WorkItem `initial_phase`. Demonstrate the graph normalization
explicitly. If one reviewed-work graph still covers the corpus, include
pre-authorized entry offsets for initial review/rereview as well as correction;
otherwise retain the smallest additional base pattern actually required. Make
the future renderer prove it can round-trip every current profile before the
grammar is accepted.

== 5. Explicit no-finding conclusions

The following areas were tested and found materially sound.

Central split and service rejection::
The concept correctly rejects one monolithic Commissioning Service. Combining
request authorship with admission/floor derivation would put the grant inside
the beneficiary. The proposed split reuses the existing Level-1/Level-0 trust
boundary rather than inventing another.

Compiler kind and placement::
Deterministic Commission compilation is an authority-free requesting-side
projection concern. It may be a library or CLI tool used by Level 1 and may be
distributed with WFC for schema proximity, but no evidence supports an
independently deployed/stateful service or new architectural level. Exact
package placement is not yet an architectural decision.

Task type, semantic role, WorkflowRole, and persona::
The four concepts are genuinely distinct. Task type and semantic expertise are
consumer/Level-1 semantics; current `WorkflowRole` is a closed phase-function
vocabulary; persona and Components are bounded prompt composition. The concept
correctly prevents role prose such as `haskell_developer` from granting path,
data, network, or provider authority.

Existing Components::
The quantitative claim is correct. Components are selected in declared order,
loaded from the pinned revision, digest-recorded, individually and collectively
bounded, and materially underused. Reusing them for reusable role/reference
prose before adding persona/harness catalogs is sound. Components influence
prompt content; they do not themselves widen scope, actions, routes, visibility,
or provider eligibility.

Evidence ownership::
The concept correctly keeps acceptance meaning above the boundary, exact
Coordinator-trusted execution/evidence mechanics below it, and trusted minimum
evidence as a non-removable floor. It does not let compiler convenience decide
what the project accepts.

Monotone assurance and capability derivation::
The stated rule is sound: request-side configuration may add assurance or
narrow a request; trusted derivation may raise assurance or narrow capability;
neither path may derive wider authority. Illegal modifier combinations still
require deterministic admission, which the concept recognizes through
pre-authorized selection and fail-closed validation.

Capability versus information escalation::
The distinction is correct. Mutation/execution elevation may require mandatory
defensive review, while protected-read eligibility must be resolved before
disclosure because later review cannot undo information flow. The concept also
correctly keys elevated handling on capability plus classified resource/effect,
not on the name `git_write` alone.

Typed escalation::
The concept correctly preserves the six current variants, rejects a wholesale
replacement ontology, keeps architecture as an orthogonal semantic/authority
classification, and treats protected-read need as the only plausible new
distinction. No schema expansion is presently justified.

Provider binding under protected information::
The observation is sound: once binding determines whether protected context may
be disclosed, the constrained binding is no longer authority-neutral and
cannot remain only an unpinned CLI-derived choice. The concept appropriately
preserves this sequencing need without prematurely choosing a policy format.

Above-Level-0 model use::
Advisory model use over unprotected input can remain above Level 0 when its
output is only an untrusted request and it has no governed effect path. Model
execution must cross the trusted boundary when it produces trusted evidence,
performs governed effects, consumes protected context, relies on privileged
capability, or becomes accepted execution provenance. Generating an intent
cannot itself manufacture authority.

Four versus five governing inputs::
The concept knows that admission pins five inputs and uses "four files" for the
four Commission-specific files alongside the shared Components input. This is
not a material architecture defect, although later text should prefer "four
Commission-specific files plus shared Components" to prevent implementation
shorthand from becoming a false model.

Level-1 openness::
The concept expressly leaves Level 1 free to grow goal custody, backlog
reasoning, decomposition, dependency and successor planning, architecture
disposition, semantic lifecycle management, and closure judgment. It does not
freeze Level 1 as an escalation classifier plus intent author.

Incremental/value-first direction::
Reusing Components and adding provider-free authoring validation are small,
independently useful first steps. A renderer remains conditional on measured
reduction. Base-pattern work must follow F-M5 and the already-recorded open
question about where pre-authorization of patterns lives; with those
conditions, the sequence remains incremental rather than a hidden platform
build.

* The base-pattern set's authority home remains unresolved. The concept
  correctly says patterns must be selected from a pre-authorized set and notes
  that trusted repository policy is the likely home. Step 4 must not place an

* The authoring-time validator will need a provider-free draft-input seam; the

  and requires a clean worktree. That is an implementation prerequisite, not a
  trust-boundary contradiction.
* Exact compiler packaging is open. Trust placement rules out a service with
  grant/admission authority, but does not decide whether a future implementation
  is consumer-local, a WFC-distributed CLI, or a reusable library.

== 7. Required amendment set

The smallest amendment set is:

. Replace unqualified `CommissionIntent = canonical truth` with the two-level
  authoring/execution distinction in F-M1 and define render/edit divergence.
. Correct the current authority statement: explicit independent maintainer
  authorization is the act; Git is the current review/transport/pin. Remove
  permanent `commit` and hand-authored-forever claims while preserving the
  separately protected authority-source invariant.
. Reopen the standing TaskAssessment fixture as an unresolved option until its
  dynamic task-input, read/context, information-flow, and provenance envelope
  is specified.
. Add trusted minimum model capability/assurance, where policy requires it, to
  the below-boundary eligibility floor; keep cost/latency/stronger preferences
  above.
. Correct the profile-corpus measurement and make the grammar cover and
  round-trip current gate-entry continuations before accepting it.

No other architectural amendment is required by this review.

== 8. Strongest counterargument

The strongest case against adopting the direction is that
`CommissionIntent + renderer` does not remove authority complexity; it creates
a fifth artifact and a synchronization problem while the generated files remain
the independently editable authority Level 0 actually admits. The renderer may
remove repeated path lists and route wiring yet still leave operators deciding
which of intent or projection is true after a manual edit. In that form the
compiler relocates complexity and creates two canonical stories.

The concept answers only half of this objection. Its measured duplication,
underused Components seam, P2/P3 authoring failures, staged metrics, and option
to stop after the validator establish a credible value case. They do not answer
projection identity or divergence. F-M1 is therefore required. With a precise
authoring-source/execution-authority split, deterministic render-equivalence,
and evidence that the stated metrics improve, the counterargument is adequately
contained. Without that amendment, it is not.

== 9. Direct answers

. *Does the core split survive?* Yes. Semantic authoring/projection stays above;
  trusted derivation/admission/enforcement stays inside Level 0.
. *Is a distinct Commissioning Service warranted?* No. No independent trust,
  state, provider, deployment, or ownership boundary justifies it.
. *Where should deterministic Commission compilation live?* On the requesting
  side of the existing trust boundary, as authority-free library/CLI tooling
  used by Level 1. Exact packaging remains open; it is not a new level.
. *Is CommissionIntent canonical authoring source, canonical execution
  authority, both, or unresolved?* It may be the canonical authoring source only
  under a defined render-equivalence rule. It is not current or minimal-future
  execution authority. Admitted governing inputs are execution authority. A
  future mechanically bound authority role remains unresolved.
. *What is the current authorizing act?* Explicit authorization by an
  independently trusted maintainer/operator of the exact bundle, operationally
  coupled today to review/commit and a separate pinned launch. Commit alone is
  insufficient.
. *Is that authorizing act an architectural invariant?* No. The invariant is a
  separately protected authority source fixed before the requester can
  influence it.
. *Does TaskAssessment require L0 when model-assisted?* Not solely because a
  model is used. It requires trusted L0 when it consumes protected context,
  produces trusted evidence/provenance, or uses governed effects or privileged
  capabilities. Otherwise it may remain untrusted advice above L0.
. *Is a standing assessment envelope actually sufficient, or still unresolved?*
  Unresolved; F-M3 identifies the missing input/read/provenance envelope.
. *Are task type, semantic role, WorkflowRole and persona correctly separated?*
  Yes.
. *Should existing Components be reused before new persona/harness machinery?*
  Yes.
. *Is workflow grammar/composition justified by current profile evidence?*
  Directionally yes, but the exact grammar is not yet justified; F-M5 must be
  corrected and round-trip tested.
. *Is assurance composition correctly monotone?* Yes at the boundary level:
  requesters may strengthen/narrow; trusted policy may strengthen/narrow; no
  request may remove the floor.
. *Who owns model capability/security floors?* Trusted policy and Level-0
  admission/derivation.
. *Who owns cost/quality preference?* The requester/Level 1, but only within
  the eligible set and above trusted minimum capability/security floors.
. *Can above-L0 LLM use remain legitimate?* Yes, for untrusted advisory work
  over unprotected inputs without a governed effect path.
. *When must model execution pass through trusted L0?* When it produces trusted
  evidence or accepted execution provenance, mutates governed resources,
  consumes protected context, or relies on privileged capability.
. *Is capability escalation correctly separated from information-access
  escalation?* Yes.
. *Must information-access eligibility be resolved before disclosure?* Yes,
  fail-closed, including ingress, egress, execution-boundary/provider
  compatibility, and declassification.
. *Does current typed escalation need expansion?* No wholesale expansion. A
  protected-read need may eventually require a predicate or kind only if it
  materially changes timing, authority owner, execution boundary, evidence, or
  successor compilation.
. *Does the concept reduce complexity rather than merely relocate it?* Existing
  Components and earlier validation clearly can. `CommissionIntent` can only if
  F-M1 is resolved and measured authoring/refusal/duplication metrics improve.
. *Does the concept leave Level 1 sufficiently open-ended?* Yes.
. *Is the minimal implementation sequence genuinely incremental/value-first?*
  Yes after the five amendments, especially the profile-corpus correction and
  explicit pattern pre-authorization dependency.

== 10. Final verdict

*ACCEPT WITH MATERIAL REVISIONS.*

BLOCKING findings:: 0.
MATERIAL findings:: 5 (`F-M1` through `F-M5`).

The core surviving architecture is:

* no monolithic Commissioning Service and no new trust boundary;
* authority-free semantic authoring and deterministic projection above Level 0;
* separately protected authorization of the exact admitted request;
* trusted, monotone capability/information/assurance derivation and enforcement
  inside Level 0; and
* advisory above-Level-0 model use only when it cannot create governed effects,
  trusted evidence, protected disclosure, or authority.

Once the five bounded amendments are applied, the concept is suitable as the
preferred non-authorizing architecture hypothesis. It is not implementation
authority.
