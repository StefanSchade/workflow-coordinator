== 1. Disposition

Outcome:: `accept`
Route:: `accept`

The bounded correction closes both stable blocking identifiers carried from
review 001.  PREPARE now independently reconstructs and byte-compares the
projection at one observed clean HEAD before canonical `_preflight`, and the
ordinary prepared launch envelope binds LAUNCH to that HEAD.  Direct-v1 and
Robust-v1 are now complete literal program records rather than deferred
catalogue sketches.  The correction weakens no existing admission predicate
and leaves no hidden Direct/Robust process decision to an Intent.

Stable blocker dispositions:: `FDC001` closed; `FDC002` closed.
Open blocking identifiers:: none.

These stable review identifiers are historical design-review requirements, not
authoritative Finding IDs in this Run.  This rereview creates no new WFC
Current-Run Finding and submits no Finding-ledger delta.

== 2. Review method

The rereview used fresh context and was bounded to the two predecessor blockers
and direct correction regressions.  It read review 001 and the corrected design
in full; compared the candidate with the predecessor-reviewed version; checked
the amended Commissioning Service trust-boundary split, the Front Door
Direct/Robust decision, and the accepted profile archaeology's counterexamples;
and walked current `operator.py::_preflight`, `_admit_a1`,
`_admit_evidence_disposition`, the profile parser/model, and the Harness
PREPARE/LAUNCH pinning behavior.

The falsification questions were concrete: whether a manifest edit could still
make a hand-edited projection pass without a rerender; whether any tracked file
claimed to contain its own commit identity; whether PREPARE and LAUNCH could
observe different candidate trees; whether either v1 program still needed a
later author to select a route, effect, action, artifact rule, evidence
placement, component, review descriptor, Finding authority, or evidence-failure
route; and whether the correction bypassed an existing admission predicate.

== 3. Stable blocker dispositions

=== FDC001 -- closed

Carried requirement (verbatim from review 001 section 6)::
`FDC001` -- replace the self-referential/digest-only pin with a concrete
PREPARE-time projection-equivalence check bound through LAUNCH.

Disposition:: *Closed.*

The corrected sections 4.1--4.3 remove the impossible manifest-contained
bundle commit.  The manifest contains source identities, target paths, and
content digests, but deliberately contains no observed bundle HEAD `H`.
For the Commission-Intent path, PREPARE must now:

. obtain the normal `expected_head` `H`, require a clean checkout at exactly
  `H`, and read the Intent and proposed governing blobs from tree `H`;
. resolve the literal program, policy, component-manifest, and selected-
  component source objects at the source commits pinned by that Intent;
. rerun the deterministic projector in a fresh temporary output from only that
  Intent blob and those pinned source objects;
. byte-compare the projected WorkItem, profile, policy, task, and render
  manifest against tree `H`, and byte-compare the reused component manifest and
  selected components against their pinned source blobs; and
. only after every comparison succeeds call canonical `_preflight` with the
  same five paths and the same `expected_head=H`.

This is an equivalence proof, not consistency with a mutable digest list.  A
hand edit plus a matching manifest-digest edit still differs from the fresh
rerender and is refused.  After the check, current PREPARE materializes its
normal launch artifact and pins it to Development HEAD `H`; current LAUNCH
re-digests the prepared artifacts, revalidates repository pins and cleanliness,
and executes that artifact unchanged.  The canonical invocation in the
artifact carries `--expected-head H`.  The proof at PREPARE therefore cannot be
silently reused for another tree at LAUNCH.

Evidence:: Corrected design sections 3 post-table note, 4.1--4.3, 6, and 9;
review 001 section 3 (`FDC001`); `operator.py::_preflight`; operator manual
sections 7--8; `scripts/wfc_op.py` prepared-envelope materialization and launch
revalidation.

=== FDC002 -- closed

Carried requirement (verbatim from review 001 section 6)::
`FDC002` -- state the complete closed Direct-v1 and Robust-v1 program
semantics, including checked Direct behavior under current gate-only
required-evidence admission and Robust's exact Finding authority.

Disposition:: *Closed.*

Corrected section 5 states that its two tables are the entire v1 grammar and
literal rendering constants.  They fix every field the predecessor review
required:

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Program |Closed semantics now fixed

|`direct@v1`
|`implement` is a `worker`/`[worker]` phase over Intent scope `S`, with no
artifact, `[edit, commit]`, no required evidence, no review descriptor, and
only `verify: goto verification`.  `verification` is a fresh worker-role gate
over `S`, with `[review]`, no artifact, exact worker component selection,
resolve-empty Finding authority, `failed_evidence_route: correction`, and only
`accept: complete` or `correction: goto implement`.  `task-check-v1` places the
one policy-basis `task-check` command on that gate; `none` places no evidence
and is explicitly described as the narrower unchecked Direct claim, not the
checked `DEC-WFC-022` target.

|`robust@v1`
|The four literal phases fix both role/component pairs, artifact and scope
bindings, actions, evidence placement, every review field, and every route key
and effect.  `implement` can only enter `broad_review`; a broad correction can
only enter `correct`; `correct` can only enter `focused_review`; and a
successful focused review can only take `renew_broad_review: goto
broad_review`.  Only `broad_review.accept` completes.  Both gates are fresh,
carry required `task-check`, route failed evidence to literal `correction`, and
have exactly `finding_authority: [resolve]`; neither may residualize,
supersede, or reopen.
|===

Every phase also fixes `human_gates: []`, modifier components, and repository-
policy components.  The two allowed evidence-profile mappings are finite; an
Intent supplies neither command argv/timeout nor arbitrary evidence placement.
No process guarantee identified by the archaeology remains an Intent parameter
or an unspecified future catalogue choice.  The finite Robust table makes the
renewed-broad-review invariant mechanically testable without designing P3.5 or
a general graph language.

Evidence:: Corrected design sections 3.1--3.2, 5.1--5.2, 6, and 9; review 001
section 3 (`FDC002`); Front Door MVP decisions section 2.4; archaeology sections
A, D, and E; `operator.py::_admit_evidence_disposition`; profile serialization
for review descriptors, routes, action coherence, and failed-evidence routes;
operator manual sections 22.1--22.4.

== 5. Evidence and acceptance boundary

evidence named by this Commission and remains the route-admission authority.
An agent-shell diagnostic run reached the Harness integration tests, whose
isolated temporary-environment builds attempted to fetch `setuptools>=68` from
the network and failed because this shell has no network access; the resulting
fixture errors and dependent failures are environmental and do not demonstrate

that restriction.  The remaining 729 collected tests passed when the affected
Harness module was excluded, and this artifact rendered successfully with
Asciidoctor.  Agent-side checks are diagnostic only and are not reported as
Coordinator-trusted evidence.

Acceptance here makes the corrected design this Commission's accepted target
design.  It does not authorize a CommissionIntent parser, renderer or compiler,
a Direct or Robust profile, historical migration, P3.5, Runtime promotion, a
Run, a provider invocation, or a push.  Each implementation remains subject to
a later, separately authorized Commission.
