[[engineering-conventions]]
== Engineering conventions

Prefer code and permanent explanations whose purpose is understandable locally,
without needing a historical ticket or review to decode them. Keep names,
errors and nearby comments clear about current behavior; link to the owner for
rationale or a larger contract instead of copying it. Apply judgment
proportionate to the change, without a readability metric or mandatory template.

Trace the contracts actually affected and choose tests that can detect a
meaningful regression. Existing feature markers can explain why a test exists;
architecture guards can protect broader boundaries. A small isolated change
may need only a focused check, while a change to a shared result or persistence
seam may need tests on both sides. Do not invent a consumer to justify a test or
migration; consider real consumers and the friction of preserving, changing or
retiring their interfaces.

Pay particular attention to producer/consumer handoffs: for example, a
Commission produces inputs consumed by admission, an adapter returns a
candidate consumed by validation, and the operator publishes state consumed by
status and later reconstruction. Ask what each side assumes about identity,
shape, ordering and ownership, and test the affected behavior across the seam.

Keep decisions in the deterministic core when practical, with filesystem, Git,
provider and evidence effects at explicit boundaries. This makes decisions
replayable and effects observable; it does not make provider output or diagnostic
text trusted. See link:{developer-docs}/architecture.md[architecture] and the
link:{developer-docs}/technical_model/workflow_program_architecture.adoc[Technical Model]
for the existing division and its limits. Maintain the owning documentation
when behavior changes, and use the
link:{developer-docs}/ai_info/doc_conventions.adoc[documentation conventions]
for placement and standing.

[[beta-contracts-provisional]]
=== In Beta, contracts are provisional: judge a change on its merits

During the Beta phase WFC owes no stability to existing schemas, workflow
programs, contracts, CLI surfaces or persistence formats; the phase exists to
find and remove unsuitable contracts and architectural couplings. The
existence of an accepted contract or of an earlier architecture decision is
therefore no argument against a change by itself. What must be preserved are
the intended safety and governance properties (for example the independent
reviewer's sole Finding authority, the trusted-commit boundary, fail-closed
recovery and admission), not necessarily their present technical realisation.
Evaluate a change on benefit, effort, risk and dependencies; defer it only for
a stated safety or authority reason, for cost out of proportion to the
benefit, or for a missing dependency. Maintainer decision of 2026-11-26.
This is a planning convention, not a gate, and it does not weaken any
decision that still requires the maintainer.

[[frontdoor-docs-sync]]
=== Keep the Agent Skill and consumer tutorials in sync with Front Door changes

On any change to the observable behavior or the operating surface of the
installed WFC Front Door (the `wfc init`/`plan`/`run`/`resume`/`status`/
`audit`/`report`/`archive`/`skill` commands), check whether the shipped Agent
Skill texts and the consumer tutorials still say what is true. The check
covers in particular: CLI commands, arguments and defaults; status and error
classifications; reports, diagnostics and usage information; admission,
execution-binding and recovery behavior; and operating flows and documented
limitations. An edit to the skill or tutorial text is needed only when one of
their statements or examples becomes inaccurate, incomplete or misleading
because of the change; a change that leaves them true needs no edit. For
example, the consumer quickstart and tutorial listings currently document
that no token usage is reported (`usage_not_reported`), while the Agent
Skill says nothing about token usage; an implementation of token accounting
would make the quickstart and tutorial statements incomplete and must
trigger the check, which should also ask whether the Agent Skill now needs
a statement about it.

The owners of this operating information: the
link:{developer-docs}/operator_manual/operator_manual.adoc[Operator Manual],
notably link:{developer-docs}/operator_manual/ch03_running/_chapter.adoc#running[chapter 3, running]
for launch and status, link:{developer-docs}/operator_manual/ch06_recovery/_chapter.adoc#recovery[chapter 6, recovery]
and link:{developer-docs}/operator_manual/ch09_consumer_quickstart/_chapter.adoc#consumer-quickstart-chapter[chapter 9, consumer quickstart and tutorials]
with its stated limitations list; the Agent Skill sources,
link:{developer-docs}/../src/workflow_coordinator/agent_skill/wfc/SKILL.md[SKILL.md]
and its `references/*.md` files, one text shared by the Claude and Codex
hosts; and the consumer tutorials, the
link:{developer-docs}/operator_manual/ch09_consumer_quickstart/[ch09_consumer_quickstart]
pages and the
link:{developer-docs}/../examples/consumer-demo/[consumer-demo demo kit], whose
quoted listings are guarded by
link:{developer-docs}/../tests/architecture/test_tutorial_kit_quotes.py[test_tutorial_kit_quotes.py].

The existing contract tests
link:{developer-docs}/../tests/unit/test_agent_skill_content.py[test_agent_skill_content.py],
link:{developer-docs}/../tests/unit/test_frontdoor_skill.py[test_frontdoor_skill.py],
link:{developer-docs}/../tests/architecture/test_consumer_tutorial_pages.py[test_consumer_tutorial_pages.py]
and link:{developer-docs}/../tests/architecture/test_tutorial_kit_quotes.py[test_tutorial_kit_quotes.py]
already pin parts of these texts, so a product change may also fail one of
them; they do not replace the check, which also reaches statements the tests
do not pin. This is a review question to ask on a Front Door change, not an
obligation to edit and not a gate.
