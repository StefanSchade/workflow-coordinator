== Durable observation

The P3.5 initiative followed a real self-development run
(`BUG-WFC-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-GATE-FINDING-AUTHORITY`) that became stranded
after a blocking Finding was opened without a viable subsequent disposition
route. The maintainer has now explicitly selected, as the intended future
P3.5 direction, a *static existential reachability property*: for a newly
opened blocking Finding, at least one actually selectable, legally
authorized route sequence must reach a suitable disposition opportunity in a
later invocation; for a carried open Finding, the current phase may itself
be a legal disposer, or one may be reachable later. This is an
existence-of-recovery-opportunity requirement; it neither predicts actual
Findings nor guarantees agent behavior, and it does not imply that an agent
will choose the repair route or successfully dispose the Finding. An
alternative path that attempts completion with an open blocker remains
rejected by the existing dynamic reducer completion guard; the static
requirement, under this selected direction, need not reject the entire
workflow merely because such an alternative route also exists.

The accepted P3.5 design instead formalizes a stronger *universal path*
condition: every relevant checkpoint-reaching continuation of an
opener-capable phase must encounter a strictly later disposer opportunity;
one uncovered continuation is sufficient for rejection.

*Corrected historical finding (2026-10-26):* the original 2025-10-25 draft of
this record characterized the universal condition as a later escalation
introduced at an unverified point after an originally existential concept,
and called for tracing "the quantifier change." The accepted
requirements-archaeology reassessment traced the actual Git history and
found a different, more precise picture:

* At `965d14d` ("docs(wfc): refine projected result contract roadmap"), the
  *first* P3.5 Concept text already contains a literal universal/every-path
  quantifier -- "some phase on *every path* to that gate" must possess the
  needed disposition operation -- conditioned on a gate whose successful
  route requires disposition.
* The *same* `965d14d` revision, in the same section, *also* states a
  conflicting existential-looking boundary: P3.5 "only rejects graphs that
  could never have reached a legal completion regardless of any Run's
  actual history." A graph with one legal repair continuation has not
  *never* been able to reach legal completion; the document never reconciles
  these two sentences.
* `f31a423` ("docs(wfc): correct P3.5 conformance criteria", citing design
  review_001 finding 4.2) did *not* introduce the first universal
  quantifier. It changed the reference point to each opening phase, made
  the required disposer strictly *later* than that opener, and generalized
  the scope to every forward checkpoint-reaching path from it -- a genuine
  ordering correction, responding to a real single-continuation
  counterexample, not the origin of the stronger-than-existential reading.
* The cited motivating incident (the S3-002 history under
  `BUG-WFC-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-GATE-FINDING-AUTHORITY`) and review_001's own
  counterexample are both single-continuation topologies. Neither
  distinguishes a profile with no later disposer at all from a profile
  offering a branch where a later disposer is legally selectable on one
  route while another route is not -- the exact case that separates the
  existential and universal readings. Minimal counterexamples establishing
  that distinguishing case are not present in the historical record.

The historical origin is therefore an *internally inconsistent requirements
record present from the first written revision*, not a demonstrated case
where one identifiable commit, model, agent, or review hop first invented a
stronger quantifier than was originally intended. Do not retrospectively
attribute the universal reading's origin to `f31a423`, to any one agent,
prompt, hop, or author; the archaeology found no such single point of
escalation and found instead that both readings were textually supportable
from the concept's own first draft onward.

== How the mismatch became visible, and how it was resolved

* The accepted-design inventory/review reported that the proposed admission
  predicate would reject 43 of 43 pre-existing schema-v2 self-development
  profiles. These are *profiles*, not phases; many belong to historical
  Commissions, and most are preserved historical-inspection fixtures rather
  than currently relevant launch targets (see below).
* The independent P3.5 structural-invariant reassessment ran on 2026-10-25
  (`WI-WFC-P35-STRUCTURAL-INVARIANT-REASSESSMENT-001`, accepted at H004,
  commit `fab606e`). It recommended deferring the accepted P3.5 package for
  Level 0 on safety/cost-proportionality grounds -- citing the existing
  dynamic completion guard as an adequate backstop and the 43/0 result as
  disproportionate for a capability-only predictor -- while explicitly
  leaving open whether the universal reading was the correctly intended
  requirement. *That deferral recommendation was not adopted as a maintainer
  architecture decision.*
* A second, independent reassessment -- the 2026-10-26
  requirements-archaeology reassessment described above -- approached the
  same still-pending question from a different angle: textual/requirements
  fidelity rather than safety/cost-proportionality. Its first attempt
  (`WI-WFC-P35-REQUIREMENTS-ARCHAEOLOGY-REASSESSMENT-001`, Run A,
  `...-2026-10-26-01`) ended `reconcile_required` after an uncertain,
  non-semantic provider-exit incident in H001 (`returncode=1`, no timeout,
  no structured result; see the operational diagnostic concern below). A
  fresh Run B against the identical Commission and baseline completed four
  Hops and was independently accepted at H004 (commit `e5af5c8f`).
* Both reassessments converged on *not* resolving the existential-versus-
  universal choice themselves, each instead sharpening what the maintainer
  specifically needed to decide, and each independently reconfirming that
  the existing dynamic reducer completion guard already prevents unsafe
  completion under either reading.
* The maintainer has since explicitly disposed of that pending choice: the
  narrower existential authority/state-aware reachability predicate (stated
  precisely in the accepted 2026-10-26 reassessment, section B) is selected
  as the intended future P3.5 requirements direction, and Front Door --
  including the Runtime/Profile/Commission architecture boundary
  clarification -- is prioritized ahead of any further P3.5 implementation
  work. See the companion Front Door planning concept and the Project
  Memory decisions it anchors.

== Failure mode and significance

*Semantic requirement escalation:* an AI-assisted design process can turn a
legitimate problem into a stronger, cleaner-looking, formally testable
requirement that the motivating use cases do not themselves justify or
disambiguate. Each subsequent independent review can then rigorously verify
internal consistency against the already-accepted formalization while
leaving that formalization's own requirements fidelity unchallenged. A
successful multi-hop, adversarial review is therefore not proof of
requirements fidelity -- it is proof of conformance to a premise the review
was never asked to re-examine. The process may then treat secondary effects
of its own overconstraint (e.g., a 43/0 legacy-profile rejection) as reasons
for major migration or broad deferral, when the more precise response is to
ask whether the stronger quantifier was ever actually required.

This record's own correction history illustrates the same failure mode one
level up: the original 2026-10-25 draft asserted an unverified escalation
narrative (a clean existential origin, later strengthened at an unknown
point) that turned out, on actual archaeological inspection, to be wrong --
the tension was present from the first written revision. A plausible,
well-written causal story about *when* a requirement was strengthened is not
itself evidence that the story is correct; it must be checked against the
actual historical record before being retained as project memory.

This resembles a suspected earlier pattern in spreadsheet-handling's Domain
Maintainer Contract (DMC): requirements and abstraction obligations can
expand through semantic deduction beyond demonstrated consumer use cases.
This remains a *cross-project analogy to investigate*, not a proven shared
root cause or a replacement for the DMC's own decision history.

== Boundaries and follow-up

* This entry now records a *resolved* historical-archaeology question (where
  the universal/existential tension entered the record: at the Concept's
  first P3.5 revision, not at a later escalation point) and a *maintainer
  decision* on which requirement direction to pursue (the existential
  predicate), together with an explicit deferral of implementation behind
  Front Door.
* P3.5 implementation itself is *deferred, not completed, cancelled, or
  declared unnecessary*. `BUG-WFC-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-GATE-FINDING-AUTHORITY`
  remains open. No code, profile migration, accepted-design amendment, BUG
  closure, or Level-0 roadmap change is authorized by this memory record.
* Do not conflate the static topology requirement with dynamic Run/Completion
  safety. The reducer's completion guard is a separate, already-verified
  stateful backstop, not a substitute for the intended structural property
  under either reading.
* Do not silently substitute the existential requirement into source or
  governance. Its exact semantics (legal reachability, phase ordering, role
  authority, cycle/path handling, and whether additional conditional route
  constraints are needed) are stated precisely in the accepted 2026-10-26
  reassessment, section B, and require a separately authorized
  implementation Commission, scheduled after the Front Door
  Profile/Commission architecture work this record's companion plan
  describes.
* The 43/0 inventory must not be re-described as a current launch census:
  of the 43 profiles at the design's snapshot commit `6807d56`, 8 are
  mandatory correction-convergence regression fixtures and 11 more are
  other historical-inspection fixtures already bypassing production
  launch-admission; the remaining 24, plus two later P3.5-Commission
  profiles added after that snapshot, have no established current launch
  demand one way or the other under either predicate. This is a concrete
  instance of the general profile/test proliferation driver discussed in the
  companion Front Door planning concept.
