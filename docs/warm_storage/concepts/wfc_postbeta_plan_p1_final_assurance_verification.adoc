== 1. Method

Maintainer decision of 2026-11-21: a fresh, independent whole-plan
verification before the plan is adopted as the basis of the post-Beta
programme. The plan was *accepted in parts only*: Run
`RUN-b49d10084a5e4a999461a3bcacdd91df` stopped `awaiting_human_budget` at 10 of
10 Hops after the last whole-plan broad review H008 and a later correction H009
that only the focused review H010 checked. This Hop therefore took **no
acceptance credit from any earlier review** and re-derived every claim from the
repository.

Base HEAD:: `6754c18338c9136874edf5c0212667802a5db6fa`.

What was read in full:: The plan (2186 lines at entry),
link:../../backlog/ROADMAP-POST-BETA-TO-RELEASE-BRIEF.adoc[the brief] (341
lines), and the cited parts of
link:../../backlog/_open.adoc[the backlog overview] (707 lines),
`docs/backlog/beta1_execution_plan.adoc`, `docs/backlog/beta1_productization.adoc`,
`docs/README.adoc`, the 47 backlog leaves' `Status::` lines, and the record
sections the plan cites by line.

How citations were checked:: Mechanically wherever the claim is countable, by
reading the exact lines wherever it is a quotation or an attribution.
+
--
. *Paths.* Every repository path appearing in the plan was extracted by regular
  expression and tested for existence; 77 distinct paths resolved. Every
  non-resolving token was then classified by hand as a bare module/file name, a
  path the plan itself declares absent or new by design, or a defect. None was
  a defect.
. *Line ranges and symbols.* Each cited range was printed with absolute line
  numbers from the file at base HEAD and compared against what the plan says is
  there.
. *Counts.* Recomputed from `git ls-files` and from parsing, never from the
  plan's own prose: the backlog census, the `governance/` census, the
  `authority: gate` / `finding_authority` census, the contract-surface counts,
  the legacy-vocabulary counts, the module line counts.
. *Commits.* Every backtick-quoted 7-hex token (35 of them) was resolved with
  `git cat-file -t` and its subject compared with the plan's attribution.
. *Run facts.* Recomputed from the Operator-staged
  `build/wfc/postbeta-inputs/beta_runs_facts.json` after verifying its SHA-256
  against `MANIFEST.sha256` with `sha256sum -c` (`OK`). `PROVENANCE.txt` was
  read; it names only `beta_runs_facts.json` as present, consistent with the
  plan's staging note.
. *Program equivalence.* `governance/workflow-programs/{robust,simple}/v1.yaml`
  was loaded and compared field by field against the imported
  `commissioning._robust_template()` / `_simple_template()`.
. *Graph properties.* The dependency table's blockers and parallelism claims
  and the five Commissions' scope lists were transcribed into a script that
  checks for cycles, missing blockers, asymmetric parallelism claims and
  scope-leaf overlap between pairs declared parallel.
. *Rendering.* `asciidoctor --failure-level=WARN` on the plan, before and after
  the corrections, plus inspection of the rendered HTML to confirm the one new
  anchor resolves.
--

Exploratory-only status:: Every command below was run in the Worker's sandbox
and is exploratory validation, not Coordinator-trusted evidence. Coordinator
`task-check` is a separate, Coordinator-owned step not performed here.

Scripts used for the mechanical checks were written under the gitignored
`build/verify/` tree and are not part of the commit.
