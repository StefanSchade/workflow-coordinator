=== 3. Blocking Finding

*B1 — The Release-notes element carries two dispositions.* The task directs:
"For every release element … record exactly one disposition: adopt as is; adopt
with a justified deviation; WFC improves on it (and name the later follow-up for
`spreadsheet-handling`); or not applicable", and the acceptance criterion reads
"every release element has exactly one disposition with evidence". The table row
at plan lines 109–120 reads `adopt + improve`. It is the only row of eleven that
does; the other ten are single-valued. The row does bundle two separable
decisions — adopting the reference's single-file release-notes home, and
improving its manual freeze into a preflight assertion — and both are argued and
evidenced, so no content is missing; what is missing is the single unambiguous
decision per element that the table exists to deliver, and a reader cannot
mechanically extract WFC's disposition for this element.

*Feasible minimal correction:* split the row into two elements —
"Release-notes home and section shape" (`adopt`, evidence
`02_release_runbook.adoc:761-799`, `04_release_history.adoc:1-3`) and
"Release-notes freeze and drift guard" (`improve`, evidence
`02_release_runbook.adoc:781-799`,
`architecture_dmc_resumption_review_2026-11-15.adoc:61-62`, keeping the named
follow-up for `release_check.sh`) — or keep one row, label it `improve`, and
state the adoption inside the decision cell. Either is a text-local change; no
decision, budget or package changes. Given the proportion guidance, offset the
added lines elsewhere rather than growing the plan (see section 5).

should decide; nothing here blocks.

=== 5. Simplicity, maintainability, and one proportion question for the maintainer

I looked specifically for mechanism that does not earn its place and did not find
it. The plan declines the reference's Pages machinery, needs zero repository
secrets, keeps one workflow file, gives the artifact rule one implementation with
two callers, folds the README guard into the preflight instead of a second tool
and procedure, and names four rejected packages with reasons — including the one
expensive alternative (a reviewed offline-input profile per runner image) that a
less careful plan would have adopted to make CI look complete. Three packages
totalling 20 Hops, eight gates and six maintainer decisions is proportionate to a
first release of an already-installable tool. The two deviations that matter most
(no required suite on the runner; static version) are each argued from a
reproduced mechanical fact, and the plan records them as decisions rather than
omissions.

*Proportion question.* The plan is 480 lines against "about 450 lines at most".
I do not treat ~7 % over as a violation of "about", and I would not have the
Worker drop a package, gate or disposition to reach 450 — the task forbids
exactly that trade. The maintainer may wish to settle whether 450 is a soft
target or a ceiling; if it is a ceiling, the compressible material is §2's
trailing paragraph and the prose in §6/§7, not any required block. The B1
correction should at least not increase the total.
