== What the candidate does

`docs/backlog/BUG-WFC-EVIDENCE-FAILURE-DIAGNOSTICS.adoc` commissions a
second, explicitly untrusted diagnostic channel alongside an unchanged
trusted `Observation`: a bounded stdout/stderr tail, populated only for a
failed trusted A2 evidence observation, never written into an existing
A2-contract field and never represented as trusted evidence.

The candidate adds a frozen `UntrustedDiagnosticCapture` dataclass
(`model.py:750-765`) carrying `stdout_tail_base64`,
`stdout_tail_truncated`, `stderr_tail_base64`, and
`stderr_tail_truncated`, and a single new optional `Observation` field,
`untrusted_diagnostic` (`model.py:793`), defaulting to `None`.
`evidence.py` gains a module constant
`UNTRUSTED_DIAGNOSTIC_CAPTURE_LIMIT_BYTES = 16_384`, a `_diagnostic_tail`
helper that base64-encodes `value[-LIMIT:]` and reports whether the
original exceeded the bound, and `_untrusted_diagnostic_capture`, which
builds the per-stream pair. `_observation` accepts the capture as a new
keyword argument and passes it through unchanged. `persistence.py`
projects the capture as a nested object (or `null`), and
`serialization.py` parses it as an optional field with its own closed
field set and base64 validation.

== Residual, non-blocking observations

* `observation_error` does not reject a capture attached to a `"pass"` or
  `"error"` observation; the fail-only invariant is enforced only at the
  single production construction site. Because the field is untrusted and
  ignored by the reducer, this cannot affect an outcome, but a future
  hardening pass could assert it at the gate.
* The capture is durable inside the A2 attempt document. That is the design
  the commission chose and manual section 16 now states it is never trusted
  A2 evidence, but the historical WFC-EVID-08 sentence "no stdout or stderr
  body shall be durable A2 evidence" reads literally against a durable tail
  of any size. The trusted summary, counts, digests, and body-omission flags
  it governs are unchanged; any clarification of that historical wording is
  a separate documentation concern.
* Captured tails may contain paths and environment-dependent detail and are
  not redacted. Extending the sibling `workflow-coordinator-run-evidence`
  archive allow-list to recognize and retain this small artifact type
  remains the follow-up the backlog record already names as out of scope,
  and it should account for up to about 44 KiB of base64 tail per failed
  observation.
* The distinct correction-cycling-with-idle-worker observation from
  `RUN-b435ab0c7e394eb5912330c38b5eca5b` remains uncommissioned and is
  untouched by this candidate.
