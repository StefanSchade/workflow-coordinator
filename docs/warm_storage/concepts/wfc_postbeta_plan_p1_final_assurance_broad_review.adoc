=== B1 -- AD-2 says one rendered `direct@v1` bundle survives; two do

*Claim under test.* Section 3.3's AD-2 evidence row: "One rendered bundle
survives (`governance/commissions/fd0-direct-v1-quickstart-001/`)"; the AD-2
record repeats it ("One rendered bundle survives at ...") and AD-2's

*What the repository shows.* Two rendered bundles under
`governance/commissions/` are compiled from `direct@v1`:

* `fd0-direct-v1-quickstart-001` -- `work-item.yaml` labels
  `program_id=direct`, `program_revision=v1`; phases `implement`,
  `verification`.
* `wfc-fd08-scratch-investigation-001` -- same labels; phases `implement`,
  `verification`; and its `render-manifest.json` pins
  `"program": {"id": "direct", "path": "governance/workflow-programs/direct/v1.yaml",
  "revision": "v1", ...}`.

Both therefore become unparseable against a missing program under option (c),
and both are rendered, not hand-authored.

*Why this is blocking.* It is a wrong count in an AD record's evidence and in
its consequence analysis, which is exactly what the acceptance criterion "every
cited path, symbol, count ... has been verified" covers, and it understates the
cost of the option AD-2 rejects by half of the affected artifacts. It is also an
internal contradiction: the matrix row for
`BUG-WFC-SELF-DEVELOPMENT-GATE-FINDING-AUTHORITY` and the `<<deferred>>` bullet
both state that 45 of the 46 empty-authority gate phases are archive material
"(43 under `self-development/`, 2 rendered bundles)" -- and those two rendered
bundles are precisely these two `direct@v1` bundles. The H001 verification
record names both by name in its own section 2.5 while reporting AD-2 as
holding, so the contradicting evidence was in hand and was not followed through.

*Minimal correction that would resolve it.* State two rendered bundles and name
the second in the AD-2 evidence row, the AD-2 record and Consequences (c). No
recommendation changes -- (b) is if anything better supported, since a second
rendered bundle is further evidence for retaining the program as historical
material.

that `PROVENANCE.txt` describes is *not present*", and M10: "`spreadsheet-handling-4acca3da/`
is described in `PROVENANCE.txt` but absent from the staged directory".

staged.

*Why this is blocking.* The commissioning task directed this Run to "read
`PROVENANCE.txt` for what is actually present", and the H001 edit widened the
`Verification basis::` line to assert that "Every citation was then re-derived
from scratch at the final-assurance base HEAD `6754c18`". Two statements in the
