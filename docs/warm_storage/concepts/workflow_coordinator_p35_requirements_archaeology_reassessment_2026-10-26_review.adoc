=== P35RAR-001 -- the central historical transition is not established from the complete original text

The candidate repeatedly says `f31a423` is the earliest supported
introduction of the universal all-path rule and concludes that its universal
portion is an escalation. The cited predecessor text does not support that
claim as written. In `965d14d`, Concept §11 already requires that "some phase
on *every path* to that gate" possess the needed operation
(`965d14d:docs/wfc_projected_result_contracts_concept_2026-10-07.adoc`, lines
408-411). `f31a423` changes the reference point to each opening phase, makes
the authority strictly later, and names every forward path from that phase;
its commit message describes this as ordered reachability "rather than path
membership," not as introduction of the first universal quantifier. The
assessment may still establish that `f31a423` broadened the trigger or scope,
but it must compare those exact quantifiers instead of equating "earliest
current unconditional wording" with "earliest universal all-path
requirement."

The omitted text makes the archaeology genuinely conflicting rather than
one-directional. The same original §11 says P3.5 "only rejects graphs that
could never have reached a legal completion regardless of any Run's actual
history" (`965d14d`, lines 429-439). That is direct existential-looking
language and is difficult to reconcile with the immediately preceding
every-path sentence. It is materially stronger evidence for the candidate's
narrowing conclusion than an absence of a branching incident, but it also
requires the assessment to acknowledge and adjudicate an internal original-
document inconsistency. Sections A and E currently omit it and therefore do
not establish that the universal wording itself first appeared at `f31a423`.

The incident characterization also conflates an observed history with its
configured topology. The BUG's H001-H003 narrative followed
`review -> correct -> rereview -> accept`, but the cited profile is not
linear: `review` has both `accept: complete` and `correct: goto correct`
(`governance/self-development/correction-convergence-escape-implementation-
s3-002-profile.yaml`, lines 5-28), and `rereview` has the same two-way shape
(lines 57-80). Neither branch has a later disposer, so this is not the
safe/unsafe branch that distinguishes the two proposed invariants; however,
calling the YAML linear is factually wrong. The BUG itself states a
purpose/authority invariant (BUG lines 77-138) and reports the one exercised
history (lines 41-67), not a proof about all branching profiles.

Correction must revise sections A, B, E, and the opening summary to:

* identify `965d14d` as the first literal every-path wording;
* separately identify exactly what `f31a423` adds or broadens;
* weigh the original §11 existential boundary sentence against its
  every-path target sentence and state whether the record resolves that
  contradiction;
* distinguish the S3-002 configured branches from the single historical
  route sequence; and
* retain, narrow, or escalate the final recommendation according to that
  corrected evidence rather than the current false transition premise.

=== P35RAR-002 -- the replacement predicate is ambiguous and contradicts its worked branch result

Section E requires "at least one legally selectable, strictly later invocation
opportunity ... before a completion attempt that would carry the Finding."
Section B simultaneously says case 3 is admitted even though its direct
`O -> C` completion attempt has no disposer before it. If "before a completion
attempt" is path-relative, case 3 fails; if it merely means that some
alternative route to a disposer is selectable from `O`, the text must say so
formally. This is not cosmetic: it is the exact existential-versus-universal
decision the reassessment exists to make.

The candidate also requires a strictly later opportunity from "each phase
that can leave one open," while its source discussion says a carried Finding
can be disposed by the phase currently entered. Current reducer ordering
applies the current invocation's deltas before its selected route
(`src/workflow_coordinator/reducer.py`, `reduce_result` Finding-delta
application followed by `_apply_route`), and `_apply_route` stops a direct
`COMPLETE` attempt with an open blocker (lines 1152-1174). The corrected rule
therefore needs to distinguish (a) a newly opened Finding, for which the
opening invocation's pre-existing authority cannot disposition the newly
allocated identity, from (b) a carried Finding, for which the current
invocation may itself be the legal disposition opportunity. It must also say
how cycles/re-entry are evaluated without turning "every phase that may leave
one open" into an unintended regress requiring another disposer after every
disposer that could decline to act.

Correction must state one implementable static predicate and re-evaluate all
worked cases against it. At minimum it must define the quantified origin/state,
the eligible current-versus-strictly-later disposer, the route/path over which
existence is tested, and why the direct disposer-free branch in case 3 is
admitted. This is requirements precision only; no implementation design or
new workflow language is requested.

=== P35RAR-003 -- the required 43-profile distinction was deferred rather than performed

Section D explicitly says the assessment did not re-derive the 43/0 count,
inspected only S3-002, and leaves classification of current usage and migration
need to a future implementation Commission. That does not satisfy this
Commission's requirement to distinguish total historical profiles, currently
used profiles, genuinely required migrations, and artifacts that need not
remain launch-compatible.

The repository already provides concrete distinctions the assessment can
verify. At accepted-design commit `6807d56`, the inventory contains 43
schema-v2 self-development profile files. Accepted-design §6.1 identifies
eight correction-convergence profiles plus eleven additional profiles as
historical-inspection fixtures that already fail the separate production
evidence-admission predicate (lines 779-811); those nineteen must not be
described as newly launch-admissible bundles becoming rejected. The current
tree contains 45 schema-v2 profile files because the prior structural
reassessment and this WorkItem added two profiles after that snapshot. The
S3-002 profile directly verifies one historical failure shape, but it does
not classify the remaining inventory or establish current launch demand.

Correction need not assume that every historical profile requires migration,
and it need not invent evidence of operational use that the repository does
not contain. It must, however, produce an evidence-backed classification to
the extent the repository supports one: preserve the 43-at-`6807d56` snapshot
as the design result; identify known historical/bypassed fixtures; identify
any profile with concrete current/new-launch relevance; state which profiles,
if any, would genuinely require migration under the narrowed recommendation;
and explicitly label any residual usage category as unresolved rather than
deferring the entire required distinction.

=== P35RAR-001 -- RESOLVED

The corrected section A now distinguishes three facts that the first candidate
conflated. `965d14d` lines 408--411 contain the first located literal
``every path to that gate'' quantifier, while lines 429--439 say P3.5 only
rejects a graph that could never have reached legal completion. The

makes the disposer strictly later, and quantifies every forward

rather than from the former false claim that `f31a423` introduced the first
universal quantifier.

The S3-002 profile check also survives rereview. Both `review` and `rereview`
declare `accept: complete` and `correct: goto correct`; neither continuation
supplies a later disposer. The assessment now accurately calls the observed
`review -> correct -> rereview -> accept` sequence the factual history while
describing the YAML itself as branching. It does not use that profile as the
missing distinguishing safe/unsafe case.

=== P35RAR-002 -- RESOLVED

The corrected section B defines the alternative predicate over an origin
configuration `(P,new)` or `(P,carried)`, a state-legal disposer, and a sequence
of declared agent-selectable `goto` routes. For a newly created Finding the
witness must be a positive-length later invocation; for a carried Finding the
current phase may be the witness. This matches current reducer ordering:
`reduce_result` applies Finding deltas at lines 1713--1721 and calls
`_apply_route` with the updated Run at line 1835; `_apply_route` then refuses
`COMPLETE` while an open blocker remains at lines 1166--1168.

Reconstructing H002's branch independently gives the same stated result. From
an opener `O`, a declared `O -> D` correction route to a legal disposer is an
existential witness, so the alternative predicate admits the profile even if
`O` also exposes a direct `COMPLETE` route. Selecting the latter route does not
complete incorrectly; the existing completion guard stops it. The universal
predicate rejects that uncovered branch instead. Orphan authority and the
S3-002 no-later-disposer topology have no selectable witness and are rejected
by both. A non-empty cycle can provide a later invocation without recursively
demanding another disposer after the Finding has actually been closed. Sections
B and E now state these quantifiers consistently and leave their policy choice
to the maintainer.
