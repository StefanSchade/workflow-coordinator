= Maintainer view (input, 2026-11-27)

Standing:: The maintainer's statements as given to the Level-1 orchestrator on
27 November 2026 (session `91a9f47c`), after reading the external LLM draft and
before the history Commission was started. Quoted in the original German with
an English gloss. They are the maintainer's interpretation and wish, not
established fact; the history keeps them attributed.

== Statements

. *On the recovery episode of 24/25 November.* The maintainer "stumbles first"
  over the recovery problems around the Level-1-autonomy run that had in effect
  been finished but had to be restarted because of budget limits, after which
  the orchestrator, in the maintainer's feeling, ran an *exaggerated governance
  loop*. The external draft describes this as an *autonomy problem*; the
  maintainer sees it rather as *governance that was too rigid*, and notes that
  the two condition each other. (Original: "ich sehe es eher als eine zu starre
  Governance - aber das bedingt sich natuerlich auch".)
. *On the weight of that episode.* The maintainer believes the draft
  *overstates its importance*, because among the many Runs so far (see the Run
  ledger) it was the exception. (Original: "ueberbetont weil es in den vielen
  runs ... die ausnahme war".)
. *On the overall impression.* Looking back on the last days, the maintainer's
  impression is that an *astonishing degree of autonomy* was reached, and that
  he would speak of an *agentic software factory*: perhaps one that is not
  production-ready in the corporate sense because aspects such as deployment or
  MCP are (still) missing, but as a *beta version* of such a factory a claim he
  would definitely put forward. (Original: "ich wuerde von einer agentic
  software factory reden ... als beta version einer solchen auf jeden fall ein
  claim den ich vorbringen wuerde".)
. *On how the history is to be written (requirement).* "Please document so
  that I can use the project experience ex post as a hard reference for
  corresponding claims (anchored in facts)." (Original: "bitte so
  dokumentieren dass ich ex post die projekterfahrung als harte referenz fuer
  entsprechende behauptungen nutzen kann (an fakten verankert)".)

== What follows for the history

* The history documents the period in a way a reader can *check*: every
  statement that could serve as evidence for a claim carries its anchor
  (commit, Run, review artifact, ledger entry), and facts are separated from
  interpretation and from the three perspectives (maintainer, orchestrator,
  external LLM).
* It must state, for each claim the maintainer may want to make, what the
  record supports, how strongly, and where it stops (see the claims register
  required by the Commission).
* The recovery episode is described by its facts and its frequency in the Run
  ledger; the competing readings (autonomy problem versus too rigid
  governance) are both attributed and neither is adopted as the history's own
  verdict unless the facts decide it.
