=== Simplicity and maintainability

This is the shape the objective asks for: repoint, repair, delete. 236 lines of
indirection removed for 49 added, no new mechanism, no state, no compatibility
layer, nothing new to maintain, and one chain of indirection — manual chapter →
root stub → manual chapter — eliminated outright. I looked for a materially
simpler alternative and found none that is not simply less work. I also looked
for added complexity to object to:

* The nine fragment retargets each pick an existing anchor instead of adding
  one. That is the cheapest possible resolution and it keeps the invariant that
  anchors belong to their owning section.
* Three of the thirty retargets use a chapter assembly file
  (`ch05_self_development/_chapter.adoc`) rather than a leaf. That is right
  where the citing sentence covers both leaves (`docs/project_memory.adoc`'s
  orientation, the adapter snapshot's "Commission-authoring … plus Runtime
  promotion runbook", the charter's "Self-development contract" row), and it
  follows the convention already used by `docs/README.adoc` and
  `docs/ai_info/_ai_info.adoc`.
* The one paragraph appended to `FTR-WFC-DOCUMENTATION-CURATION.adoc` is not
  "documentation of the documentation": the section it follows asserted "The
  five root paths remain because concrete current readers still need them" and
  "Those five stubs and their anchors are unchanged", which the candidate makes
  false. It mirrors D1's dated note exactly, preserves the original C4
  narrative and table, and changes no `Status::` line or acceptance claim. Nine
  lines is proportionate for a statement that would otherwise mislead.
* The one leaf repaired beyond the plan's literal 14
  (`ch01_orientation/01_model.adoc`) was the right call, not scope creep: it sat
  inside the already-granted `docs/operator_manual/` directory scope and held a
  live present-tense parenthetical naming three of the five paths, which
  deletion would have turned into three dangling citations in the manual's own
  orientation leaf.

=== Proportion note for the maintainer

I am not waiving anything; everything the task requires reproduces. One
criterion is worth the maintainer's attention for D3–D4 rather than this
package: V4's second form requires "every `docs/` hit a dated provenance
sentence named in the package result". For D2 that is 72 hits in 23 files, and
for D3 the prefix `docs/warm_storage/concepts/` will produce an order of
magnitude more. Enumerating them per package buys little once the directive
form reads zero and `check` shows no new unresolved link, and it reliably
produces incomplete lists (observation 1). Restating the expectation as "no
`docs/` hit outside a dated or self-dating record, and any exception named" would
keep the useful signal — a live present-tense citation to a removed path — at a
fraction of the reporting cost. That is a plan-drafting matter and does not
affect D2's acceptance.
