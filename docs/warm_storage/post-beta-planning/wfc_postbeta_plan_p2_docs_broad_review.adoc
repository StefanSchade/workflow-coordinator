=== 4. Simplicity and proportion

I looked for a materially simpler plan with the same guarantees and did not
find one. The four packages separate the three real risk classes — pure
deletion, judged fragment retargeting, bulk rename — plus a small cleanup, and
each dependency has a concrete reason (a shared `docs/README.adoc` edit; a
destination that must exist; a path that must not move while it is being
retired; and for D4 the two maintainer answers D3 does not need). Folding D4
into D3 would make a 138-file rename wait on MD-2 and MD-3 for no gain. The
nine rejections in §9 are the right ones, and the taxonomy is derivable from
the filename, which is what keeps it free of an index to maintain. The
verification reuses `asciidoctor`, `wfc_doc_catalog.py` and `git grep` and adds
nothing.

At 558 lines the plan is within the Operator's raised ceiling, and the material
I would cut first — §2.2's per-theme split and §5.2's per-shim consumer lists —
is exactly what the acceptance criterion requires and what makes the packages
checkable. I am not asking for compression.

=== 2. Withheld authority and the maintainer principles are honoured

The range `f8e8e5e..c0e8408` changes one leaf, the plan. No move, rename or
deletion; nothing under `src/`, `tests/`, `governance/` or `project_memory/`; no

Against the binding principles: the plan proposes no redirect file, stub,

system, register, header, field or governance process; the single new file is

=== 3. Simplicity and proportion

I tried to find a materially simpler plan that keeps the same guarantees and
could not. The four packages are cut along the three real risk classes —
pure deletion (D1), judged fragment retargeting (D2), bulk rename (D3) — plus a
small cleanup (D4), and each depends on its predecessor for a concrete reason
(a shared `docs/README.adoc` edit; a destination that must exist; a path that
must not move while it is being retired). Dropping D1 or D4 into D3 would mix a
pure deletion or a judged retarget into a 138-file rename, which is the one
thing a rename package should not carry. The nine rejected packages in §9 are
the right rejections, and two of them — a README per warm group and a per-file
mapping table — are exactly the mechanisms a weaker plan would have added. The
verification reuses `asciidoctor`, `wfc_doc_catalog.py generate` and `check`
and `git grep` and adds no script, make target or report.

The one place where cost may exceed benefit is a maintainer question, not a
defect: D3's scope takes six model and manual directories plus `docs/backlog/`
as whole directories because the inbound links are spread across 28 files. That
is honest about where the links are, but it is a wide scope for a rename
package. I record it as an observation rather than a Finding because the plan
states the reason and the alternative (per-leaf scope across 28 files) is worse
to author and to check.
