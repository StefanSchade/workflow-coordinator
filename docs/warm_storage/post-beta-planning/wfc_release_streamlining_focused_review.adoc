=== 2. New blocking Finding — the ownership rule stops at the conditional branch the plan recommends

*Invariant.* A slice's owning documentation is true when that slice lands
(`ch03_engineering_conventions.adoc:36-37`, "Maintain the owning documentation
when behavior changes"), and the candidate's own rule that *every* slice owns
the chapter lines its own work makes true (plan `:496-506`), enforced by `R2`
`AC-9`: "no statement of that kind is left out of the table".

*What the files say today.* `python-compat` is informational by a recorded
maintainer decision: `release.yml:288-295` ("no release gate (no job waits for
it; it reports the compatibility facts for the maintainer)"),
`publish-testpypi`'s `needs: check` (`release.yml:293-295`),
`ch04:164-166` ("not a prerequisite of any publishing job") and `ch04:566`
("the informational `python-compat` matrix").

*What the candidate specifies.* `R3` adds `python-compat` to
`publish-testpypi`'s `needs` *if and only if* the maintainer takes `D6` Option
A (plan `:681-686`, `R3` `:198-209`, `AC-5`). The plan *recommends* Option A
(`:700-708`). `R2` meanwhile writes the chapter so that it describes the job
"exactly as the workflow and the recorded 2026-11-23 maintainer decision
describe it ... an informational job that is not a prerequisite of any
publishing job and not one of the gates G1-G8" (`AC-3`, `:271-278`; design row
`:161-165`; non-goal `:135`), unconditionally and with no "planned" marking,
because `AC-3` also states that "the chapter does *not* announce a `needs:`
change".

*The reproduced consequence.* On the recommended branch, the moment `R3`
integrates, chapter 4 states that a job which can stop the publication chain
cannot — and the gate list `G1`-`G8`, whose scoping `R2` is asked to make
explicit, then omits the only thing outside it that can block a release. No
slice may repair it: `R3`'s scope says "Nothing else in chapter 4 is in scope"
(`:125-127`) and `AC-8` pins "No line of the chapter outside the four
statements of `<<scope>>` differs; `git diff` on that file shows nothing else"
(`:243-251`); the ownership table has five rows and no row for it; `R2`'s
`AC-3` and non-goal hold the opposite text in place; and the plan's autonomy
list gives the Level-1 operator upkeep in the controlling records and
`_open.adoc`, not in a developer-guide chapter (`:570-574`). `R3` can still fix
the workflow's *own* comment at `release.yml:288-295`, because that file is in
its scope — which makes the asymmetry plain: the same slice is required to keep
the YAML comment honest and forbidden to keep the release chapter honest.
I searched all six candidate documents for `python-compat`: nothing anywhere
assigns the chapter consequence of Option A, and the only sentence that comes
near it covers the period *before* the decision (plan `:710-712`, "Until D6 is

lands in the document a maintainer reads at the `pypi` approval pause, about

recommends, not on an unlikely one. It is *not* a new requirement of mine: the
rule, the table and `R2` `AC-9` are the candidate's own, and `F005`'s
correction applied them to every unconditional statement.
