=== 2. New blocking Finding

[[n1]]
==== N-1 (ID allocated by the Coordinator) — the summary count of the version-bearing surface is wrong and self-contradictory

The enumeration is exact; the count derived from it is not. The R1 record's
table (`FTR-WFC-RELEASE-PREPARATION.adoc:40-90`) lists, in its *Site* column,
thirteen distinct files holding 37 line sites:

. `pyproject.toml:7` (1)
. `src/workflow_coordinator/implementation_identity.py:9` (1)
. `tests/architecture/test_bp1_identity.py:41` (1)
. `README.md:17-22`, `:24`, `:26`, `:34`, `:46`, `:56`, `:59` (12)
. `src/workflow_coordinator/agent_skill/wfc/SKILL.md:140-145` (6)
. `…/references/commission.md` (1), `…/references/setup.md` (2),
  `…/references/stops-and-evidence.md` (3) — 6 over three files
. `docs/release_notes/release_notes.adoc:9` (1)
. `docs/operator_manual/ch09_consumer_quickstart/01_quickstart.adoc:120`,
  `:126`, `:130`, `:133` (4)
. `…/02_first_run_example.adoc` (2), `…/03_tutorial_program_change.adoc` (2),
  `…/04_tutorial_stops.adoc` (1) — 5 over three files

1+1+1+12+6+6+1+4+5 = *37 sites*, in *13 files*. I re-read every one of those
lines in this checkout; all 37 are real and all line numbers are exact. But
the candidate says "eleven files" in eight places:
`beta2_release_streamlining_plan.adoc:87` ("hand editing across eleven
files"), `:234` ("an eleven-file hand edit"),
`FTR-WFC-RELEASE-PREPARATION.adoc:92`, `:117` ("the completeness of a list of
eleven files"), `:174` ("Three rules suffice for the eleven files"),
`wfc_release_streamlining_planning_record.adoc:107`, `:172`, and
`_open.adoc:200` ("about 37 sites in eleven files, not four sites in four").

Two things make this blocking rather than a nit:

. *The record contradicts itself with the correct number.*
  `FTR-WFC-RELEASE-PREPARATION.adoc:246` says that until `D2`/`D4` is decided
  the slice should "implement the other ten files and leave the five lines and
  the test as they are". Ten is right — 13 minus the three chapter 9 tutorial
  pages. It is arithmetically impossible together with a total of eleven,
  under which the three excluded pages would leave eight. No reading
  reconciles the two numbers: excluding the two files handled by a different
  rule (`release_notes.adoc`, frozen rather than rewritten, and
  `test_bp1_identity.py`) reaches eleven only by also dropping two of the 37
  sites, so "37 sites in eleven files" is wrong in both of its halves at once.
. *The count is the slice's own completeness measure, and this Commission
  exists to correct exactly this kind of undercount.* The R1 record's economic
  argument is that "the recurring cost is the completeness of a list of eleven
  files, which is exactly what a human forgets and a list in code does not"
  (`:116-118`), and the design builds "one module-level table … Three rules
  suffice for the eleven files" (`:173-174`). A Worker who implements that
  table against the stated count, rather than against the enumerated table,
  builds a short list — the single failure mode R1 is designed to remove. The
  same wrong number is what the maintainer reads in `_open.adoc:200`, directly
  beside the true observation that the basis undercounted ("not four sites in
  four").

A second quantitative claim in the same analysis also fails against the
repository, and belongs in the same correction:
`wfc_release_streamlining_planning_record.adoc:100-102` and
`FTR-WFC-RELEASE-PREPARATION.adoc:106-108` state that "roughly forty"
`pre-0.1.0b1` references occur across `docs/`, and that "a naive grep for the
version string over-reports by about a factor of three". Measured here over
`docs/**/*.{adoc,md}`: *252 lines in 81 files* contain `pre-0.1.0b1` (275
occurrences; 312 counting all file types), and a naive search for `0.1.0b1`
across `docs/` matches 660 occurrences in 145 files against the ten
version-bearing documentation sites the table lists — a factor far from three,
whichever unit is chosen. The *refutation itself is correct and valuable* (the
prefix is a directory name, not a version pin, and `wfc_doc_catalog.py:762`,
`:875` do name `docs/cold_storage/sources/pre-0.1.0b1/…`); only the two
magnitudes are unsupported.

Correction (bounded, documentation-only, inside the authorized scope):: state
the file count as the enumeration supports it — 13 files holding 37 line sites
— in the eight places listed above, keeping `:246`'s "ten files" as it is; and
either replace "roughly forty … factor of three" with the measured figures or
drop the two magnitudes and keep the refutation. Nothing else in the candidate
depends on either number: the per-site enumeration, the two new gaps that
carry W2's economic case, the slice boundaries, the guards and the acceptance
criteria are all unaffected.
