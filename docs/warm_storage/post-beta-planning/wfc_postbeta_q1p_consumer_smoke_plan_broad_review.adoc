=== 3. Blocking Finding -- the new passages do not render as the record they claim to be

*Invariant.* A changed documentation page renders correctly, and a decision
recorded in the `decisions` register renders as an entry of that register
(`docs/ai_info/doc_conventions.adoc`: "Render changed pages and relevant
assemblies"). The measured check reported for the candidate -- a strict render
that exits 0 with no output -- is true and yet proves nothing here: the plan is
only linked from `docs/backlog/_open.adoc`, never included, so the G5 render
set does not cover it at all, and AsciiDoctor emits no warning for any of the
four defects below. I rendered the page itself and read the output.

Reproduction: `asciidoctor --safe-mode=safe -a assembled -o -
--failure-level=WARN docs/backlog/postbeta_plan_p3_release_engineering.adoc`
(exit 0, no diagnostics), then reading the HTML.

. *The `MD-6` entry drops out of the decisions register.* Its
  description-list term is split over two source lines: line 632 ends with the
  bold "decided" plus a comma, and line 633 begins "revised 2026-11-23" and
  carries the double colon. A term must end with the double colon on one line,
  so AsciiDoctor closes the register list after `MD-5` and renders `MD-6` as a
  trailing paragraph. Measured in the HTML: `MD-4` and `MD-5` render inside
  `dt` elements, `MD-6` inside a `p` element, with the double colon left as
  body text. The list-continuation marker on line 650 then has no list item to
  continue and renders as a paragraph consisting of that bare marker, in front
  of the `Superseded (2026-11-22)` sentence, which is thereby no longer part
  of the `MD-6` entry. This is a regression: before the change the term was a
  single line and rendered inside a `dt` element like its five siblings.
. *Six lines of the open decisions render as monospace code.* On line 436 the
  code span around the sibling-repository path is closed by a backtick that is
  immediately followed by an apostrophe and an "s". A constrained span cannot
  close there, so the span runs on to the next backtick in the file, the one
  in front of `v0.1.0b1` on line 441. In the HTML, the remainder of (b) and
  the opening of (c) are wrapped in a `code` element, and a bare backtick
  reaches the reader in front of `v0.1.0b1`. The identical trap already bit
  `ch02_code_navigation.adoc` at line 30 once, which is why it is worth
  fixing here rather than reproducing.
. *Two further stray continuation markers.* Lines 419 and 431 use a
  list-continuation marker in a context that is not a list, producing one
  paragraph that contains nothing but that marker in front of the two-option
  open block, and a leading marker on the paragraph after it. Three such
  markers reach the reader in total, counting the one from defect 1.
. *(a), (b) and (c) are not a list.* With no blank line in front of the bold
  (b) and (c) lead-ins, the closing sentence of (a) and the whole of (b) and
  (c) collapse into a single paragraph, so the subsection the task requires to
  list the items as open and undecided renders as one run-on block.

Why this blocks rather than being a style note: the affected text is the
`MD-6` entry itself and the open-decisions subsection, which is what the
WorkItem produces and what the maintainer is meant to read before taking
decisions (a) to (c). One of the four defects is a structural regression of an
existing register entry, another silently typesets prose as code, and the
correction costs four local edits with no new mechanism. Nothing about the
recorded decision, the labels or the open questions needs to change.
