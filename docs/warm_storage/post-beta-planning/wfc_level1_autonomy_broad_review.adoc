==== F001 (new, blocking) -- SKILL.md and AU-5 still withhold the operator's own, already-dispositioned worktree/branch cleanup without the qualifier the task requires and chapter 10 already states

The task's own DO item 2 requires that the operator may, without a further
human decision, "clean up its own temporary artifacts precisely (own
scratch, own worktree, never someone else's data, never Evidence, never
preserved refs)"; DO item 3 states the real, narrower boundary to keep:
"discarding branches, worktrees, stashes or preserved refs the operator
*did not itself create for this task*" (`task.md` DO-2/DO-3; the Consumer
Request's own task text carries the identical qualified sentence verbatim).
Operator Manual chapter 10 implements this correctly in three places: the
standing-instructions template lists, among freedoms needing no further
human decision, "remove a worktree and its own scratch directory once that
Run is archived and its result is integrated or otherwise dispositioned"
(`01_operating_pattern.adoc:189-190`); its human-decisions list withholds
only "discarding any branch, worktree, stash entry or preserved ref Level 1
*did not itself create for this task*" (`01_operating_pattern.adoc:447-448`,
and again at `:204-205` in the standing-instructions template); and its own
worked loop performs `git worktree remove` and `git branch -d` on the
operator's own just-integrated branch as an ordinary, non-gated step
(`01_operating_pattern.adoc:327-332`).

`SKILL.md`, the actual packaged operating guide a Level-1 session loads and
follows, was not brought into line with this. Its `Authorization` rule 5
("Never delegated by a launch") and its "Always stop and ask before" list
both still read, unqualified: "discarding a branch, worktree, stash or
preserved ref" (`SKILL.md:50-51`, `:61-62`) -- identical, word for word, to
the pre-candidate baseline (`git show 5b34c01:.../SKILL.md`), i.e. this
sentence was never revised by either attempt at this Commission despite
sitting squarely inside the DO item 1 search instruction ("over-restrictive
rules for temporary artifacts, worktrees and ordinary Git operations").
Nothing elsewhere in `SKILL.md` narrows it: "The loop" section does not
describe the integration/cleanup step at all (it stops at "treating it as
accepted"), and the `Never` list does not mention worktree removal. Read on
its own terms -- which is how an operating guide must be readable, per the
task's own requirement that "the freedoms [be] equally binding (an operator
that still asks for ordinary things is a defect)" -- this sentence still
requires asking a human before the operator removes its own, already
fast-forwarded worktree and branch, exactly the technical, already-delegated
step chapter 10 and DO item 2 both say needs no further decision.

`docs/backlog/FTR-WFC-AGENT-SKILL-DISTRIBUTION.adoc`'s AU-5 carries the same
unqualified sentence -- "discarding a branch, worktree, stash or preserved
ref. These need the human each time, *as chapter 10 lists them*"
(`FTR-WFC-AGENT-SKILL-DISTRIBUTION.adoc:372-377`) -- which both omits the
qualifier and misstates what chapter 10 actually lists (chapter 10's
corresponding sentence is qualified; AU-5's is not). This is precisely the
"contradictions between skill, Operator Manual and governance" DO item 1
asks the inventory to find, and DO item 4's instruction to "keep the skill,
the manual's Level-1 chapter and the AU authorization contract consistent"
is not met for this one rule.

I checked this is not merely a stricter, deliberately-accepted design choice
inherited from the unrelated prior skill-design Commission: a historical
review record (`wfc_postbeta_b1_skill_s1_final_broad_review.adoc:210-214`)
does note the skill was deliberately kept stricter than chapter 10 on this
exact point, "because the FTR is, at AU-5" -- but that was a review of a
*different* Commission's AU-5 text, written before this Commission's task
required AU-5 itself, and the chapter it specifies, to delegate the
operator's own post-integration cleanup. This Commission's own task document
supersedes that earlier design choice for this one clause; it does not
survive as an unstated exception.

This reproduces a named acceptance criterion violation, not a stylistic
preference: DO item 2's required freedom is absent from the one document a
Level-1 session actually loads as its operating guide, and the Acceptance
section's "skill, manual and AU contract are mutually consistent" is false
for this clause. I classify it blocking per the standing instruction that a
reproduced violation of an explicit acceptance criterion is not downgraded
for low presumed risk or low correction cost; the fix here is narrow (add
the same qualifier chapter 10 already uses, in `SKILL.md`'s two sentences
and in AU-5), well inside this Commission's own scope
(`src/workflow_coordinator/agent_skill/wfc/`, `docs/backlog/FTR-WFC-AGENT-SKILL-DISTRIBUTION.adoc`),
and does not touch any of the Withheld-authority items.
