= Incident Investigation Report -- `/tmp` tmpfs Exhaustion (2026-10-28/29)
:toc:
:toclevels: 2
:sectnums:

Status:: INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT. Factual record of an observed
operational incident and its immediate diagnosis. Non-authorizing: it
authorizes no implementation, no root-cause attribution beyond what was
directly observed, and no BUG closure. It is not a design document and not a
Commission.
Date:: Incident observed and diagnosed 2026-10-28/29-2026; this report
written 2026-10-29.
Scope:: The `/tmp` tmpfs exhaustion that interrupted Run
`RUN-c358b544417c4a33a0d040a0d8c38656`
(`WI-WFC-FRONT-DOOR-DIRECT-V1-IMPLEMENTATION-002`), and the preliminary
forensic baseline captured in response. It does not establish the retention
cause of the accumulated `pytest` temporary files, and it does not authorize
or perform any cleanup, repair, or correction.

== 1. Summary

`/tmp` -- a 5.7 GiB `tmpfs` -- was completely full while the root filesystem
(`/`, `/dev/sda1`, 468 GB) had over 400 GB free. `/tmp/pytest-of-stefan`
accounted for the full 5.7 GiB across 528,603 files, concentrated in six
`pytest` session directories (216, 222, 227, 228, 229, 230). Inode usage on
the `/tmp` tmpfs stood at 607,722 of 1,048,576 (58%). The exhaustion
interrupted the Front Door Direct-v1 successor Run mid-flight at its sixth,
final Hop, after five productive Hops had already reached a trusted
implementation commit. The Run did not complete; it stopped
`reconcile_required`. The maintainer captured a preliminary forensic
baseline and, as an immediate operational measure, remounted `/tmp` at 12
GiB. No cleanup of the `pytest` directories and no repair of the Run state
have been performed. The underlying retention cause is not established by
this report.

== 2. Observations (directly established)

These facts are drawn from direct inspection of the live filesystem, the
preserved forensic baseline
(`~/wfc-diagnostics/tmp-pressure-20261029/evidence/baseline.txt`), and the
Run's own persisted Harness/Coordinator state
(`~/wfc-state/wfc-front-door-direct-v1-implementation-002-20261028-01/`).

=== 2.1 Filesystem state

* `/tmp` is mounted as a `tmpfs` of `size=5935004k` (5.7 GiB),
  `nr_inodes=1048576`.
* At capture time, `/tmp` was `100%` full (`5.7G Used`, `4.0K Avail`).
* `/` (`/dev/sda1`, 468 GB total) had 418 GB free (`6%` used) -- the
  exhaustion was specific to the `/tmp` tmpfs, not to disk space generally.
* Inode usage on `/tmp`: `607722` used of `1048576` (`58%`) -- inode
  exhaustion was not reached, but a majority of the tmpfs's inode budget was
  already consumed by the same material occupying its byte budget.

=== 2.2 Composition of `/tmp` usage

* `/tmp/pytest-of-stefan` alone accounted for `5.7G` -- effectively all of
  the full tmpfs.
* That total was distributed across six `pytest` session directories:
  `pytest-216` (685M), `pytest-222` (1.3G), `pytest-227` (504M), `pytest-228`
  (1.4G), `pytest-229` (1.4G), `pytest-230` (510M).
* The combined file count under `/tmp/pytest-of-stefan` was `528603` files.
* Recurring test-environment and repository-copy material is visible under
  these session directories (for example repeated virtualenv-shaped and
  repository-checkout-shaped trees), consistent with `pytest` fixtures that
  materialize a fresh environment or repository copy per test or per
  session. The exact fixture(s) responsible, their individual contribution
  to the 5.7 GiB/528,603-file total, and why six sessions' worth of material
  survived concurrently rather than being cleaned up, are not established by
  this report (see §4).

== 3. Plausible explanations (not yet confirmed)

* Recurring `pytest` fixtures that materialize a fresh virtual environment
  and/or a repository checkout per test or per session are a plausible
  structural source of the volume observed, consistent with the visible
  shape of the retained material (§2.2). This is consistent with, but not
  independently confirmed against, the separately recorded
  link:../concepts/workflow_coordinator_front_door_stage1_commission_test_burden_finding_2026-10-25.adoc[Front
  Door Stage 1 Commission test-burden finding]'s observation that
  `test_self_development_governance.py` alone performs 42
  `_materialize_bundle()` calls (>=252 Git subprocesses) per full suite run
  -- a different, already-documented concern about test count and runtime,
  not about temporary-file retention, but a plausible contributor to the
  same directory tree.
* Six concurrently present `pytest-NNN` session directories suggest either
  that `pytest`'s own `tmp_path`/`tmpdir` base-temp retention policy (which
  by default keeps a bounded number of recent sessions) had not yet rotated
  out these six, or that something prevented normal rotation/cleanup. Which
  of these applies is not established.
* The specific fixture(s), test module(s), or invocation pattern(s)
  responsible for the bulk of the 528,603 files, and whether any single
  session is disproportionately responsible, are not established -- the
  captured baseline records aggregate sizes per session directory, not a
  per-fixture or per-test breakdown.

== 4. Open questions

* Which specific fixture(s) or test(s) create the bulk of the retained
  files, and why.
* Why six sessions' worth of material persisted concurrently rather than
  being rotated out or cleaned up (whether by `pytest`'s own retention
  policy, an interrupted run, a configuration difference, or something
  else).
* Whether the six retained sessions correspond to six genuinely distinct
  local test invocations, or to some smaller number of invocations that
  each left more than one session directory behind.
* Whether this is a steady-state accumulation pattern that will recur
  without intervention, or was a one-off buildup.
* Whether other local processes (outside `pytest`) also write to `/tmp` in
  volumes that matter for this budget; this report did not establish that
  they do, nor that they do not.

== 5. Measures taken

* `/tmp` remounted at 12 GiB (from 5.7 GiB), as an immediate, preliminary
  operational mitigation. This is a capacity increase, not a fix for
  whatever causes retention, and does not by itself prevent recurrence at a
  larger scale.
* Forensic baseline captured to
  `~/wfc-diagnostics/tmp-pressure-20261029/evidence/baseline.txt`.
* No cleanup of `/tmp/pytest-of-stefan` was performed.
* No repair, resume, or reconciliation action was taken on Run
  `RUN-c358b544417c4a33a0d040a0d8c38656`'s `reconcile_required` state. Its
  accepted implementation at commit `b70481a` remains available, unaltered,
  as ordinary Development-repository state for the maintainer to resume,
  reconcile, or build on through a separate, explicit decision; no such
  decision is made or needed by this report.

== 7. Non-claims

This report does not claim: that the `pytest` fixture(s) responsible have
been identified; that `pytest` itself, as opposed to a specific fixture or

result was `accept`); or that any corrective action has been taken beyond
