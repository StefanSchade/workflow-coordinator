[[n3]]
=== N3 -- The retained repository-local archiving option is restated as something else and then disposed of nowhere

The epic records the Evidence decision as two parts:

____
"Prefer a separate Git-based Evidence repository as the default, *while
retaining repository-local archiving as an option*. This preference conflicts
with the older repository-local preference in `DEC-WFC-023`; reconciliation
remains open and that historical decision is not rewritten here."
(`beta1_productization.adoc:56-59`)
____

The plan renders that decision as one part:

____
"*Evidence default:* a separate Git-backed Evidence repository with a sanitized
audit projection and a *local-raw* option is preferred (answers Q3's default;
exact retention/projection fields remain open, see the plan's decision-gate
table)."
(`beta1_execution_plan.adoc:62-65`)
____

These are two different mechanisms, and the plan's own primary source says so
explicitly. In the second-pass review's Q3 row, the recommended answer is
"separate repository with a committed audit projection and *local raw files*",
and the *alternative* is "*Payload-local*: archive commits on working branches,
and BP6's privacy property becomes a release blocker, not a default"
(`wfc_beta1_product_boundary_review_second_pass.adoc:1622-1626`). The same
review states that "the separate Evidence repository reverses the stated
preference of `DEC-WFC-023` (repository-local `.wfc/archive/`)" (`:46`).
`evidence.raw: local | commit | discard` (`:472`, `:772`, `:957-960`) only
decides whether raw bytes are committed into the separate Evidence repository;
it is part of the default, not the retained alternative to it. The maintainer
retained the alternative; the plan substituted a setting of the default.

The substitution is not confined to one bullet. Checked against the whole plan,
repository-local archiving appears in no Commission and in no disposition:

* BP5's deliverable and modules (`:263-274`) cover `frontdoor/archive.py`,
  `wfc archive`, staging, atomic placement, idempotent retry and the narrowed
  cleanliness rule, with no destination parameterization -- although the epic's
  own BP5 investigation line asks to "parameterize destination/OPS, staging and
  copy-to-commit retry" (`beta1_productization.adoc:229-231`), which is where a
  second destination class would be delivered or declined.
* BP6's modules and acceptance (`:402-429`) exercise only the separate
  repository: "With `raw: local`, `git ls-files` in the Evidence repository
  shows only `audit.json` files and `.gitignore`" (`:428-429`), taken from the
  review's BP6 acceptance (`:1376`).
* DG-2 offers the maintainer "raw-file retention default (unbounded local
  versus a cap), and destination layout *under `WFC_HOME`*" (`:695-704`) --
  both alternatives presuppose the separate-repository location, so the
  maintainer is never offered the destination question the epic left open.
* The post-Beta list (`:565-571`), the "not open, and not reopened" list
  (`:755-758`) and the "explicitly not required for `0.1.0b1`" list
  (`:798-804`) do not mention it either, and nothing anywhere records that the
  `DEC-WFC-023` reconciliation the epic calls open is still open.

Why this is material rather than editorial:

. *It drops a capability the controlling decision retains.* The plan is the
  document that turns the 2026-11-18 decisions into commissionable units, and
  its basis section claims to do exactly that without re-deciding them
  (`:11-20`, `:48`). A BP5 or BP6 Commission authored from this plan would
  build a single-destination archiver and a single-destination audit
  projection. The retained option would not be refused by a maintainer; it
  would simply never be visible to any implementer -- the failure mode the
  epic's own BP9 entry names ("must receive explicit dispositions, not
  disappear because R2 omitted them", `beta1_productization.adoc:312-313`) and
  the one F003 was opened and resolved for in the Q5 and public-internet cases.
. *It silently takes the stricter-for-the-consumer reading of an open item,
  which this plan elsewhere refuses to do.* The plan's own
  `preservation-gate-status` note
  (`:71-84`) is the plan's own model for this situation: name both readings,
  state which one the plan follows and why, and give the maintainer the choice
  with a latest decision point. The Evidence decision gets none of that
  treatment, even though the epic marks the `DEC-WFC-023` reconciliation as
  explicitly unresolved.
. *The review's own alternative carries a release-gate consequence.* Under
  payload-local archiving, "BP6's privacy property becomes a release blocker,
  not a default" (`:1626`). So the omitted option is not cosmetic: whether it
  is in scope changes what BP6's privacy acceptance has to guarantee and when.

Remedy, small and entirely within the plan: correct the basis bullet to state
the decision as the epic states it (separate Git-backed Evidence repository as
the default, repository-local archiving retained as an option, `DEC-WFC-023`
reconciliation open), and then dispose of that option once -- either by naming
a configurable destination in BP5's modules and BP6's acceptance, or by adding
it to DG-2 as an explicit alternative with a latest decision point before BP5
starts, or by listing it among the explicit post-Beta items. Any one of the
three closes this; the plan need not pick the maintainer's answer, only stop
losing the question.
