== What this is, and what it is not

Historical reviews stay where they are and keep their original meaning. A reader who finds one of
their findings can mistake what the reviewer concluded _then_ for a defect that exists _now_, or the
reverse: later work may have addressed the point, replaced the mechanism, consciously left it alone,
or never established its status. This register annotates a small, evidence-checked sample of such
findings so that a reader can follow the original review, the later evidence and the commit at which
the assessment was made, and judge for themselves.

* *Not authority.* Nothing here is read by the Coordinator, admitted as a Finding, route, review
  disposition or acceptance, or cited as a reason to change code. `maintainer_accepted` is `false` on
  every entry and stays so until the Maintainer says otherwise.
* *Not a Run Finding ledger.* An entry about a historical finding says nothing about whether a Run
  Finding existed. Run Findings are Run-local, never transfer between Runs, and are untouched. Several
  sampled reviews state outright that they created none (see <<non-run>>).
* *Not a deficiency list.* An entry that says `still_relevant` records that a stated condition still
  holds at the assessed commit; it does not say the condition is a defect, and no code change follows
  from it automatically.
* *Not closure.* `addressed` means a specific later change exists and a check shows the specific
  condition no longer occurs. It is not acceptance of the whole feature, not product readiness, and
  not a statement that anything is complete.
* *Read in context.* The original review is the source for what was found. Follow its link before
  relying on a one-sentence summary here.

== Walkthrough for a reader

The path below is the one the pilot was built to support. Commands run from the repository root.

. *You hold a historical review.* Ask the derived catalog which documents link to it. The catalog is a
  local, ignored, non-authoritative file; generate it explicitly with `make doc-catalog` or use the
  in-memory projection with `--live`:
+
[source,shell]
----
python3 scripts/wfc_doc_catalog.py query --live \
  --refs-to docs/warm_storage/concepts/wfc_knowledge_catalog_k1_001_broad_review.adoc
----
+
This register is among the results for every review linked below (verification: <<k1-discovery>>).
. *Find the entry.* Entries are keyed by the review document and the finding label exactly as the review
  writes it. A bare label such as `B1` is ambiguous across reviews; only the pair is a usable key.
. *Read the original.* The `Review` line of the entry links the real document, at the finding's heading
  where it has one.
. *Check the evidence yourself.* `Evidence` names the later change (commit, test, source line, planning
  text); `Check` says what was done to see whether the condition still occurs. Run the named test or read
  the named lines.
. *Respect the commit.* `assessed_at` is a point in history. Newer commits may change the picture;
  nothing refreshes an entry automatically. For fresh leads use the read-only provenance search:
+
[source,shell]
----
python3 scripts/wfc_doc_catalog.py provenance --review docs/warm_storage/concepts/wfc_knowledge_catalog_k1_001_broad_review.adoc
python3 scripts/wfc_doc_catalog.py provenance --work-item WI-WFC-KNOWLEDGE-CATALOG-K1-001
python3 scripts/wfc_doc_catalog.py provenance --coverage
----
+
Its output separates _observed_ facts from _derived_ candidates and lists unlinked material. It
proposes leads; it never concludes that anything was addressed.
. *Decide.* The annotation is evidence for your judgment, not a replacement for it.

Worked example (entry <<e01>>): K1's broad review reported that a hash appearing only under a
`Reviewed candidate` header matched an identity query. The entry names the correcting commit and a
regression test. To reproduce the original claim, extract the reviewed candidate's script and documents
(`git archive d2eb5cd68f94f2c2dc37619eb1a1787a32ee80cc scripts docs` into a scratch directory) and run its
own `query --live --id 83ec2b10966392af281dcd22b2cc8a25109d4880`: it returns the simple-v1 broad review.
Run the same query at the assessed commit: it returns nothing, and
`pytest tests/unit/test_wfc_doc_catalog.py::test_unsupported_definition_labels_end_supported_declarations`
passes.

== Disposition vocabulary and entry fields

The six dispositions, the evidence rules and the guards are owned by
link:../../backlog/FTR-WFC-OPERATIONAL-PROVENANCE-PILOT.adoc#_disposition_vocabulary_and_evidence_rules[the pilot record];
this table is only a reading aid.

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Disposition |Used here when
|`addressed` |A specific later change is cited and a test or a source reading at the assessed commit shows the specific condition no longer occurs.
|`superseded` |A cited, verified successor or removal made the subject moot. Not used in this sample.
|`not_substantiated` |The claim was checked and not reproduced. Not used in this sample; every checked claim reproduced at its own commit.
|`not_pursued` |A cited, recorded decision or rationale consciously leaves it; this never says the finding was wrong.
|`still_relevant` |A source check at the assessed commit shows the stated condition still holds.
|`unknown` |Evidence is absent, partial or cannot be judged with the rules. A permitted final state.
|===

Every entry carries: the review link, the finding label as written (or, where the review wrote none, a
locator, said so), a one-sentence own-words summary, the disposition, the evidence and the check,
`evidence_strength` (`demonstrated` = a test run or reproduction/source reading directly tied to the
condition; `indicated` = source reading or a later review statement without a direct probe; `none`),
`assessed_at`, `assessor` and `maintainer_accepted: false`. Entries use the words "addressed" and
"concluded" for effects; the stronger closure words are deliberately avoided.

The assessor of every entry is the same: agent Run `RUN-bda29ff3fa9b415f8007ebf3416fbadb`, Hop `H001`,
WorkItem `WI-WFC-OPERATIONAL-PROVENANCE-P1-001` (worker, `claude_cli:claude-sonnet-5-5:high`). Agent-side
tests and probes named below are exploratory validation, not Coordinator-trusted evidence.

[[gaps]]
== Limits, and what the rules could not express

* *Sample, not census.* 12 reviews and 28 findings out of about 155 review-like documents. Nothing here says anything
  about other reviews, and absence of an entry is not evidence about a finding.
* *One assessed commit.* Entries age. Nothing refreshes or invalidates them; the catalog is likewise not refreshed
  automatically.
* *Compound findings.* A label such as `B4` or `H006-R4` can contain several claims; one disposition per label is
  coarse. Entries chose findings whose claims could be checked, and said where only part was.
* *"Adopted into the plan, not built".* Design-review findings adopted as planning constraints (`C3`) fit none of the six
  values. They were recorded as `unknown` with an explanation.
* *Non-defect findings.* Observations, exploratory-review corrections and design points (`C4`, `C5`, `OPUS-S4-004`,
  `OPUS-S4-005`) are not defects; `still_relevant` and `not_pursued` describe a condition and a decision, not fault.
* *Unlabelled findings.* Some reviews write no identifier (M2). A locator was used and stated.
* *Later evidence inside the original document.* Several reviews carry their later renewed-review section in the same
  file. It was treated as a lead, not as proof; entries rely on source, tests and commits.
* *Agent evidence only.* Tests and probes ran in an agent sandbox without network and with a read-only `/tmp`; the
  Claude adapter conformance tests could not run there (`OSError: [Errno 30]`). They are exploratory, not
  Coordinator-trusted evidence, and independent review plus Maintainer acceptance remain separate.
* *Unused classes.* `superseded` and `not_substantiated` had no qualifying case in this sample.
* *No automatic derivation.* No script writes a disposition; `provenance --review` only lists leads (cited paths
  present or absent at the candidate commit, later commits on them, inbound links).

== Pilot report and go/no-go

*Numbers.* Mechanical: 69 Commission directories, 858 commits, 318 with a subject token, 52 more with a body mention;
nine real WorkItems compared with manual `git log` and `grep` (all commit lists equal, one explained document
difference). Curated: 12 reviews, 28 entries (21 `addressed`, 3 `still_relevant`, 3 `unknown`, 1 `not_pursued`); 24
entries `demonstrated`, 1 `indicated`, 3 `none`. Effort: one implementation Hop (this Run's `H001`); mechanical
extension and tests were moderate, the curated sample was the larger share of effort because every entry needed its own
source or test check, the commit identified and, for compound findings, a decision about what could honestly be claimed.

*Recommendation: continue narrowly; do not widen yet.*

* *Keep* the `provenance` subcommand: it is cheap, read-only, tested and reproduces the manual joins; it states its limits.
* *Keep this register* as a warm exploratory document and add entries only on demand, for example when a reader or a Run
  meets a historical review through `--refs-to` that has no entry and a recorded open BUG or FTR links to it.
* *Do not* attempt bulk or exhaustive disposition: judgment cost per entry is high, entries age, and 49 of 155
  review-like documents show no recognizable verdict shape.
* *Before any canonical promotion*, the Maintainer should decide the open questions (a value for "adopted into the plan",
  compound findings, staleness policy). Promotion into Project Memory or the Evidence repository requires its own
  decision (D3); this pilot does not authorize widening.
* *Reasons:* the mechanical half is reliable where Git and Commission records exist and honest where they do not; the
  curated half showed that most sampled findings were demonstrably addressed by identifiable later changes, that a few
  conditions remain by design or by recorded deferral, and that some cannot be judged with the current vocabulary. The
  value is real but local, and the cost does not justify a global effort on this evidence.
